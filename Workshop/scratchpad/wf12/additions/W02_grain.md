## 3 · Traps the register should name (A22, continued)

1. **`ROOT×3+!HOLD` is not "the roots let go."** The validator reads the ligature as the compound `ROOT+HOLD` (*ralthein*, remember, A14), mirrored: "not remember", that is *forget*. Cut `!HOLD →` the thing let go, `→with ROOT×3`.
2. **A modifier is read as the doer.** `MORROW+GROW` reads "the morrow grows"; the future is a `→in MORROW` runner instead (above).
3. **The grey's file must take act heads only.** A thing-sign first on file 11 (`FLASH`, `THIN`) introduces "hard light" or "a thin one" as its referent for the rest of the round (§11.2), and every later mark of the sky reads as that. `FLASH+OPEN` and `THIN+LIVE` keep the grey.
4. **`SQUARE` on a `CASTLE` file is a belonging** (A16's built place's stones): the mute thing on the havens' file reads "the havens' stone". Give it a file of its own.
5. **A19 orders the marks a memory ray needs.** The ray leaves the first mark of its file in its year, so the remembered mark is written first in its knowing (the ranks in r5, `!PILLAR` in r21). Where the telling's order puts another of our marks first in that year (r23's *forty summers* before *the axe in our rings*), the ray cannot be cut and the relation is carried by runners.

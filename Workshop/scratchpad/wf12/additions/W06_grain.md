## 3 · Findings (for the validator and the spec; none blocks the unit)

1. **A fully italic paragraph in a wood leaf's story is not a ring** (the Legends builder's `p.inscr`, set apart from the plates). V.4's carving, *Grow until the shore is silent.*, is the second case after IV.4's sentence (W03): each is cited by its root in a cut-pocket (§8) and stands under its own round. V.4 is twenty-three paragraphs and twenty-two rings. *Proposed:* §11.2 item 1 says so ("a fully italic paragraph that is a carving or a sentence cited whole is its own round, not a ring").
2. **mystaeri_spec §5.8's GROW round, in GN v2.** Its v1 header said `wood: green (a young silverbark, standing)`; GN v2 has no comment in a header, so V-4-grow is `wood: green; pith: round; rings: 2; rules after: []`, the gloss in its `# file` lines. The validator reads it as a telling (A10) and lets green wood carry its two rings; §3's table gives green wood one ring, so a line in A10 ("a telling's rings follow the telling, not its wood") would settle it.
3. **Every cut-pocket reads "an older carving, cited"** (W05's finding 2, again): ring 17's pocket holds the *first* carving of the war, cut that night. *Proposed* as W05 proposed: "a carving: […]", keeping "an older carving, cited" for a pocket that holds a half-size root *with* an ordinal or a ray to an earlier carving.
4. **An act names no referent, so war never has a file of its own** (`STRIKE×3`, the register's *war*, is an act). The wf8 round's war on file 13 read, with no `# file` comments, as the Aelvaren's. This round puts war on the council's file (9), where it reads as the long-lived's own war, which is what the Book means. A16b, or a register row naming `STRIKE×3` on a file of its own as "a war" when it stands first, would let a later unit choose.
5. **A ligature whose head is an act names no referent** (`TREE×3+KNOT`, `TIDE@1+BENEATH`): the wf8 round's trees and first ring were lost from their files in a blind reading. This round names the thing first (`TREE×3`, `TIDE@1`) and cuts the act after it. Worth a line in §11.2 beside A16.
6. **The lone AXE reads "fell with iron"** (§21.9.6, item 6, the verb-first gloss): ring 17's *blade* is the iron, held (felt) by the tree, and felled nothing. A noun-first gloss for a mark that governs nothing would help.
7. **`ROOT×3` reads "the root-men (roots)"**: the validator's gloss of the plural root leads with the unit-type. In a grove's file it is the roots (W04 ring 21, here ring 8).
8. **The heart ring holds eight years on one file** (the grove, file 8), because v3's *as they keep all they hear* is the silver elders' own custom and is cut on their own file. §4.2 sets the heart ring's floor (72 units) and no ceiling; the radius is about 1,418 units for 38 years.
9. **What the blind round still reads wrong** (A16 only, no `# file` comments; A16b would mend each): file 12 is "the left hand" (A16b (c), the teller); file 11 is "thin one (thin wood)" for the sky (A16b (d), the band as a thing); file 5 in ring 16 is "a great anger" (the Book's own unsaid *whose*); file 3 in ring 22 is "a young silverbark" for the carving in it, which the ray to ring 18 and ring 18's runner to its `CARVE` carry.

## 4 · After the blind back-translation (`wf12/backtrans/W06.md`)

Scored in `wf12/units/W06.md`, BACK-TRANSLATION. Two changes to the round and one to the Orrowen; no compound, sign or rule added.

1. **Ring 8**: `9: CHOOSE` added to the knowing r8·4, with `run h7 5.r8·4 →for 9.r8·4`: the young ask, at the keel, *for* the council's choosing (*put the question*). Read before as *asked [questions], at the keel*. The table row *put the question* (§2) says how.
2. **Ring 6**: runner f4 springs from the counsel's `KNOT` (7.3.r6·2), not from `MOUTH{still}` (7.2.r6·2): *a waiting because of the same wound*. Read before as *even so, struck as they were, they still spoke for waiting*. The table row *the very X for which* (§2) says why.
3. **The Orrowen headnote** (W06-V4-03), not the grain: *hos ganna wrodow* > *hos ganna wrodowath*, "one gate of mouths", the pair of mouths; the singular was heard as one mouth between them.

The round is now 139 marks in the rings and 100 runners (was 138 and 99); the years (38), the cells and the radius are unchanged.

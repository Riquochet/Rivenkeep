## 3 · Findings (for the validator and the spec; none blocks the unit)

1. **H05 caps a round at 24 rings** (v1 §3.2). v3's V.6 has 25 story paragraphs, so ring 23 holds the groves' paragraph and the Song its colon introduces (as §11.3 first planned the Song: the groves' SING `→that` seven said-pockets). If the cap rises to 25, the Song can stand as its own ring under our HOLD again, as the wf8 round had it.
2. **A band may wrap** (`files 12–0`): the validator reads a band clockwise across the axis (`conds_at`), and §13.2's grammar allows it; §3.6 and the renderer should say so, and draw it as one band.
3. **The literal reading drops the plural under an ordinal** (`TREE×3@1` reads "the first tree") and doubles the plural of a part (`TREE:boughs×3` "many crown of boughss"). Glosses only.
4. **The compounds still gloss the dropped names**: `BOND+ROOT×3` "(Aelralen)", `CARVE+BEARER` "(Sethvaren)". v3 took the names out of the Book (notes_v3 §1.14); a reading for the Original tab should drop the brackets for V.6, or Jack may keep them as the wood's own words.
5. **A blind of place reads "whither" for every to-runner** (A5), though A15 says "what" for a seeing; a carving's blind would want "what" too. V.6 avoids it (finding in §2, *we know not*).
6. **A20 again**: in a celled year a band covers every mark on its file, so ring 5's WATER band lies on `!US` and ring 11's DREAD band on *many minds*. Both are true here; a validator note would catch the case where it is not.
7. **The self-runner** (`→ @f` to its own file in its own year) is exempt from R15 (`!GIFT → @9`, ring 4; `HEAR → @13`, ring 8). The renderer must find the terminal a place beside its source when the year's cells are full.
8. **§18's `!MOUTH → HOME`** ("no voice among us can call us home", [v1]) is read *do not speak to home* by a reader who does not take §18's row (the blind back-translation, ring 22). A15 could carry it: *call (one) home* is MOUTH `→` HOME, the call's aim, not the one spoken to.

## Part 4 · Findings for the unit

1. **Mutation slips the analyzer cannot see.** The analyzer skips mutation checks on its small words (`gor`, `o`).
   - W01-II.2-04 *hy gor dholm*: by §3.3 the softening after *hy* would give *hy yor dholm*.
   - W01-II.2-07 *ul o hossol*: the nasalising after *ul* would give *ul mo hossol*.
   - Neither blocks the reading. The grammar states no exemption for small words, so one of the two (the text or §3.3) should say which is right.
2. **The analyzer does not read W01-01.** Even with the row merged into the lexicon, *demo* glosses as O0396 "until-3SG.M", because the conjugated-preposition parse wins. A reader without the additions file would hear "made until it".
3. **`>TRUE` in r21** reads "are true" in the validator. Only A15 gives "make sure".
4. **Contamination of the blind test.** The allowed sources quote older II.2 Book wording:
   - `grain_v2_full.md` §7.2, §8, §10.9 and §21.9.2–3 (the "Where" columns), for example "Our land lay flat beneath the trees", "as the storm speaks in the high boughs", "You call them the Mystholders", "the black pillars at the edge of the world";
   - `W01_grain.md`, which quotes this round's ring-3 Book sentence.

   I read the signs by their Means and readings, not by those quotations. Still, a stricter blind test would hand the reader a copy of the spec with the "Where" columns stripped.
5. **The two texts agree.** The tongue's dialogue (lines 04–11) and the grain's band I tell the same things:
   - old before stone;
   - the grey among the trunks, which is not fog but breath;
   - the trees breathing in sleep;
   - the between-light, neither day nor dusk;
   - young made for that light;
   - pale trees singing at dusk;
   - the people's own name.

   The match supports the readings ⟨?⟩ above in rings 3–5 and 22: *gannath* as trunks, and the WHITE band as "pale".

## 1 · SEREN'S FRAME, IN ORROWEN (THE LEAF-HAND, HER ONE INK)

### 1.1 The title

> **Reskowath strom sa orrant um wadh**

VI.2 · The Thrones That Walk the Sea

Word for word: *seats great REL(+S) go-3PL upon(+S) sea.*

- The lexicon's title (O2093), as the wf8 unit wrote it for v1.3's III.2: "the great seats that go upon the sea". The leaf moved to Book Six and kept its name, so its Orrowen stands; the lexicon row's "III.2" is the old place (NOTES, 1).
- *Orrant*, "they go, they walk", is *orr* "go, walk; pass" (canon). The reading takes the verb up twice, bedded (*ul morrol*, walking): the Thrones walk the sea, and the shore walked on the Thrones (§2).

### 1.2 The dateline

> **Lo dhrenn et surr vranast, cumm eth cumm.**

*Through the sixth year, haven by haven.*

Word for word: *at(+S) S·course the year sixth, haven and haven.*

- **"Through" is the lexicon's *lo dhrenn***, "by the course of" (O1217), as in *lo dhrenn et grem*, "all winter, through the winter" (O2955): *trenn* softened after *lo*. **It is a word that works twice.** *Trenn* is the course a year runs and a course of stones, and the guild's word for a tale (Seren's *Nath gemment et trenneth*, "the tales do not agree"): the year ran its course, and the leaf is laid as a course is laid, a stone to a haven.
- The ordinal follows its noun, *et surr vranast*, "the sixth year" (*surr*, canon; *vranast*, the ordinal of *vran* "six", O2801), as W04's dateline does (*et syst sullast*).
- **"Haven by haven" is *cumm eth cumm***, "haven and haven", built as the lexicon's *reskow strom eth reskow strom*, "throne by throne" (O3008), and *hos eth hos*, "one by one". v1.3's colophon said *throne by throne*; v3's dateline says *haven by haven*, and the Orrowen keeps one shape for both.

### 1.3 The headnote

> **Lo reskowath strom ommat, gor hos, re vellor hos hy {{Halyna}} ul molt et mesketh, eth re ston ullen lo hless, lo sest et hesperdeth, sa re nahebbent orrol virr; eth re heth et hos hyo o hosen re heth et hos ulo. Doss molt reskow strom kethet ul hos hoss, eth et voll dasken, eth duss sost.**

*At each fallen Throne one of {{Halyna}} went among the timbers, and the other stood at the edge with the soldiers, that would not go nigh; and the one outside knew it as the one within. A Throne's heart is known in a breath, its name last and hardest.*

Word for word: *at(+S) thrones fallen, every(+S) one, PST(+S) S·come-in one from(+S) Halyna in(+N) heart the timbers, and PST(+S) stand other at(+S) S·edge, at(+S) shoulder the soldiers, REL(+S) PST(+S) refuse-3PL going near; and PST(+S) S·hold the one out-of-it it as PST(+S) S·hold the one in-it. Is heart throne held in(+N) one breath, and the name last, and hard very.*

- **Most of the first sentence is the wf8 headnote's, word for word**: *re vellor hos hy Halyna ul molt et mesketh* ("one of Halyna went in among the timbers": *mellor* "come in, enter", softened after *re*), *lo hless, lo sest et hesperdeth* ("at the edge, beside the soldiers": *cless* "a raised edge" softened after *lo*; *lo sest*, "at the shoulder of", the lexicon's "beside", O1218), and its close, *eth re heth et hos hyo o hosen re heth et hos ulo* ("and the one outside held it as the one inside held it", with *keth* "hold", the canon's verb for knowing wood, and the conjugated prepositions *hyo*, *ulo*). v3 cut *turn and turn about*, *a Throne each* and *for the Thrones were fearful even dead*, and the Orrowen cuts them with it.
- **"At each fallen Throne" is *lo reskowath strom ommat, gor hos***, "at the thrones fallen, each one": *gor hos*, "apiece, each one" (O0763), set after its noun, as S09 sets it (*dem muldath et hald, gor hos*). The thrones are the title's own, *reskowath strom*: the seats that walked the sea are the seats fallen. *Ommat* is the participle of *omm*, which is "fall" and also "fail; falter" (canon): every throne there had fallen, and had failed. **Back-translation fix** (§BACK-TRANSLATION): the line was *lo gor reskow strom ommat*, which set *gor* under *lo*'s softening; §3.3 would wear it to *yor*, pilot I.1 §3 leaves a trigger before *gor* unsettled, and the other units step round it (S02, S05, S07, S08, S09, S10, S11, W01). *Each one* also gives *hyo* and *ulo*, "out of it", "in it", their one throne, where the blind reader could not tell the throne from the trees.
- **The headnote counts in ones.** *Hos*, "one", stands in it five times, and is another one each time: each one of the thrones (*gor hos*), one of Halyna (*hos hy Halyna*), the one outside and the one within (*et hos hyo … et hos ulo*), and the one breath a heart is known in (*ul hos hoss*); and between the outside and the within it hides a sixth time, in *hosen*, "as" (O0991, "one" + *-en*, "of one": the same), for the two knew it as one. It is the leaf's own arithmetic: ten thrones, one at a time; one name for two, one of whom goes in; and a whole heart in one breath.
- **"Stood" is *ston***, "stand (of a wall), hold firm" (canon): the one outside stood as a wall stands, and the word is the close of every oath, *Ston.*, "It stands". v1.3's other one *waited* (*re dhovv*); v3's *stood* gives Seren the pun, and she takes it.
- **"That would not go nigh" is *sa re nahebbent orrol virr***, "who refused the going near": *nahebb*, "refuse", is "un-give" (O1523), and the soldiers would not give their nearness. The 3pl is slender, *-ent*, as *hebb* is. (The wf8 headnote had *did not go near*, *nath re orrant virr*; v3's *would not* is a refusal, and the Orrowen says so.)
- **"Among the timbers" is *ul molt et mesketh***, "in the heart of the timbers" (the lexicon's "among", O2633), and the next sentence's *molt reskow strom* is the heart that one went in to: one word, twice, the way in and the thing found there.
- **"A Throne's heart is known in a breath"** is the wf8 *Doss molt reskow strom kethet ul hos hoss*, "a throne's heart is held in one breath" (*ul hos hoss*, O2985, of a thing known quickly). *Hos hoss* chimes, and *hoss* is breath and life both, so the tongue hears "known in one life" under "known in a breath". **"Its name last and hardest" is *eth et voll dasken, eth duss sost***: v3 folds the wf8 sentence (*Darr voll et reskow strom dasken …*) into a clause with its verb left out, as the English leaves it out, and *et voll*, "the name", points back to the throne, where *o voll*, "its name", could not show its softening (orrowen_v2 §3.5).

## 2 · THE READING, IN ORROWEN (HALYNA, IN THEIR TWO INKS)

*As the Book sets it under `<!-- READING -->`: seven lines, each opening with an em-dash, the two inks alternating line by line and neither named. The reading is "ten breaths, one sense to a Throne, and the name fails" (notes_v3 §1.5): each Throne gave up its sense as one of Halyna stood among its timbers, so a line holds one Throne's breath or two, in the order of the havens, and the Orrowen's fragments are mostly nouns, with the progressive's verbal nouns for "walking" and "answering" (orrowen_v2 §3.4, the doss left out, as IV.6's reading does).*

*(first ink)*

> **— Nynth. Lesteth, nalelv umor.**

*— Water. Houses, heavy on us.*  

Word for word: *— water. houses, heavy upon-1PL.*

- Thaesaen's breath: its harbour, and the houses its kin held up (§3.4). *Nynth* is plain water (O1839, "a spring; water", as in *helvnynth*, rain, "sky-water"); *lesteth*, "houses" (*lest*, slender plural); *nalelv*, "heavy", is "un-light" (O1557), IV.6's word.
- **"On us" is *umor*, upon us, in the plural**: the wood's *we*, not Halyna's dual (*umona*). From the first breath the pair speak as the Throne they hold, and the slip into the wood's voice is not caught (notes_v3 §1.5). A weight, like a feeling, is laid *upon* the one who bears it (orrowen_v2 §3.7).

*(second ink)*

> **— Misketh, ul morrol umor. Lilvyl, ul et sedh.**

*— Feet, walking on us. Rot, in the fen.*  

Word for word: *— feet, in(+N) N·going upon-1PL. rot, in(+N) the fen.*

- Leavaren's breath and Vaelress's, in one ink. *Misketh*, "feet" (*misk*, slender plural). **"Walking" is *ul morrol***: *orrol*, "going, walking", bedded after *ul* (a broad vowel takes *m-*), the progressive with its *doss* dropped, as IV.6's *ul vorrol*. *Orr* is the title's own verb: the Thrones walk the sea, and here the shore walked on the Thrones.
- *Lilvyl*, "rot; rotting" (O1210), from *lilv*, "soft; go soft"; *ul et sedh*, "in the fen" (*sedh*, canon; Fenholm is *Sedhnell*).

*(first ink)*

> **— Meskrivullath, ul morrol. Tolm, ul dessyl.**

*— Roots, walking. Stone, answering.*  

Word for word: *— roots, in(+N) N·going. stone, in(+N) N·answering.*

- Ralensaen's breath and Rhenvael's. **"Roots" is *meskrivullath***, "tree-foundations" (O1389, *mesk* + *rivull*: a root as a builder names it), so the line says **foundations, walking**, which no Stonewright can picture and which is the wood's plainest truth: the pillar walked home on its roots. *Ul morrol* comes again from the other ink's line, and the two inks walk in step.
- *Tolm, ul dessyl*: *tessyl*, "answering" (O2514), bedded after *ul*. *Tess* is also "stand surety for; owe", and *tessyl* "a confession, an answering for a deed": the stone answering is the mountain's answer to the guns, and an answering for the felling.

*(second ink)*

> **— Vorrdhuss. Domm, ul vimm et hal.**

*— Ash. Dark, under the rock.*  

Word for word: *— ash. dark, below the bedrock.*

- Esthaer's breath and Senneir's. *Vorrdhuss*, "ash", is "fire-dust" (O2783); *domm*, "black; dark; the dark" (canon). **"Under the rock" is *ul vimm et hal***, "in the low of the bedrock" (*ul vimm*, O2639): *hal* is the living rock, and the canyon's rock was the living rock our kin held up.

*(first ink)*

> **— Sulv. Fedh sell ul et sulv.**

*— White. A long while in the white.*  

Word for word: *— white. time long in(+N) the white.*

- Eirlenth's breath, the one Throne given a whole line. *Sulv* is "snow; white" (canon), said twice, as the English says it. *Fedh sell*, "a long time" (*fedh*, "a time, a season"; *fedh lyss*, a short while, O2930). **Under it lies Eirlenth's name in Orrowen, *Grem Sell*, the Long Winter** (O2945): the wood says *a long while in the white*, and a Rivenman hears *the long winter* (§3.7).

*(second ink)*

*— Sky, lifted. Glass —*  

Word for word: *— sky, lifted. glass —*

- Naelthar's breath, and Neivaere's begun. *Bemmet*, "lifted", the participle of *bemm*, "lift" (IV.6's *re wemm*): the cranes lifted the stones and the towers into the sky, and the sky was lifted with them. *Helv* is the sky (canon).

*(first ink)*

> **— cresket. Vollath, ul vimm gor yalat. Thae — [ ]**

*— broken. Names, under all. Thae — [ ]*

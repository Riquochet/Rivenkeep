## 1 · SEREN'S FRAME, IN ORROWEN (THE LEAF-HAND, HER ONE INK)

### 1.1 The title

> **Redul ul vimm et Helv Bynt**

V.4 · The Council Under the Thin Sky

Word for word: *council in(+N) low the sky narrow.*

- The lexicon's title (O2048), as the wf8 unit wrote it: *ul vimm*, "in the low of", is the tongue's *under* (lexicon §5.1), and *bynt* is "narrow; fine, thin". The Book sets it as the leaf's heading; the English above is the heading's words.
- The reading's second line says the title's last two words again, *Et helv bynt*, "the sky thin": Halyna name the leaf without knowing it is a name.

### 1.2 The dateline

> **Et grem gemmest, ul Hyll Lodhat.**

*The fourth winter, in the Joined Tide.*

Word for word: *the winter fourth, in(+N) Tide Joined.*

- A naming, with no verb, the house dateline of v3's wood leaves (W03's *Et heth sullast, ul Hyll Dhumol.*, W04's *Et syst sullast, ul Hyll Dhumol.*). The ordinal follows its noun: *gemmest*, "fourth" (O0717, *gemm* + *-Ast*, slender after *e*).
- *Hyll Lodhat*, "the Joined Tide", is the lexicon's and every unit's (Tier 3 report §5.2). The nasalising *ul* leaves *h* as it is. *Lodhat* is the mason's word, "mortared, bonded", the word of the Bonded (*Lodhan et Kethow*): the Tide the hulls came joined in is named as a wall is.

### 1.3 The headnote

> **Re hadh Rhyna garl um et molt sy, eth re nahadh ey o ul hos hoss; re rarr Halvard dem susk hy et nelter, eth re yal lern thenth lo. Ew clemel et kethyl; ullen et hyll ull, et brodhol; hy vodh doss prenereth mest ul redul, eth re yal hos ganna wrodowath lonta.**

*Rhyna laid a hand on this heart, and took it away at once; Halvard came up from the vault with his face wet. The knowing took a moment, the telling the rest of that Tide, for a council is many minds, and they had one pair of mouths.*

Word for word: *PST(+S) S·lay Rhyna hand upon(+S) the heart this, and PST(+S) unlay she it in(+N) one breath; PST(+S) S·come Halvard until(+N) height from(+S) the vault, and PST(+S) S·be face wet at-him. Was moment the holding; the-rest the tide that, the telling; because be minds many in(+N) council, and PST(+S) S·be one gate S·mouth-PL at-3DU.*

- **The verb that works twice.** *Re hadh … eth re nahadh*: *cadh* "lay", softened after *re*, and *nahadh*, *na-* with *cadh*, the reversative ("unlay, take down", lexicon): the hand she laid, she unlays. *Cadh* is also the hearth's verb for a tale (*Cadhom o hosen re yal cadhat lom*, I.1), so Rhyna lays a hand on the heart as a tale is laid at the hearth, and takes it up again.
- **"At once" is *ul hos hoss*, "in one breath"** (O2985, "at once, of a thing known quickly"), where the wf8 unit wrote *pynt sost*, "very quickly". *Hoss* is breath and life: the knowing was in her for the space of one breath. *Um et molt sy*, "on this heart": v3 puts the hand on the heart, and drops the wf8 unit's shingle.
- *Re rarr Halvard dem susk hy et nelter* is the wf8 unit's (*darr* "come" softened; *dem susk* "up"; *nelter* "a vault"). ***Eth re yal lern thenth lo***, "and a wet face was at him", is the "at" idiom for having (§3.7): *o lern* could not show the possessive before *l* (§3.5), so the wf8 unit's way stands. v3 sets no *and* before Halvard's clause; the semicolon is the English's, and the *eth* after it joins the face to the coming, as the English *with* does.
- **"The knowing took a moment, the telling the rest of that Tide"** is one copula said once and laid twice: *Ew clemel et kethyl; ullen et hyll ull, et brodhol*, "a moment was the knowing; the rest of that Tide, the telling". The copula sets its predicate first (§3.4, *El tolm et hald*), and the second clause leaves it out, as the English leaves out *took*. *Ullen*, "the rest, the others" (O2649), heads a construct with a definite possessor and takes no article (§3.2): it gives back the *rest of* that the wf8 unit had to leave to the day-count.
- ***Kethyl* works twice**: "a holding" and, of the wood, "a knowing" (§3.7). The knowing was a moment because Rhyna's hand held the heart a moment.
- *Hy vodh doss prenereth mest ul redul*, "for many minds are in a council", is the wf8 unit's: the state verb, because the copula would set *prenereth mest redul* side by side and read as a construct, "the many minds of a council". *Redul* is the title's first word.
- ***Eth re yal hos ganna wrodowath lonta***, "and one gate of mouths was at them two": *ganna*, the old dual "the two posts", is the canon's word for a pair ("a pair is a gate"), and the Bonded are named as a gate is made (§3.10); *lonta* is the dual, "at them two" (§3.6). The possessor is plural, *brodowath* softened as a possessor (*b* > *w*, §3.3; the construct, §3.2): a gate of mouths is a pair of mouths, two mouths for many minds. With the singular *wrodow*, "one gate of mouth", the blind reader heard *one mouth between them* (BACK-TRANSLATION, W06-V4-03). Seren's turn is in three numbers: *mest* against *hos*, many minds and one gate; and the gate is two mouths, as *lonta* is two.

## 2 · THE READING, IN ORROWEN (HALYNA, IN THEIR TWO INKS)

*As the Book sets it under `<!-- READING -->`: eight lines, each opening with an em-dash, the two inks alternating line by line and neither named. Halyna speak in the present, in fragments, mostly nouns. In V.4 the reading takes the two sides of the council: the second ink holds the old voices' word, *Wait*, the first ink turns it, and the reading breaks where the voices are too many to hold.*

*(first ink)*

> **— Gendeth hosen crisur, feth ul vimm. Hellur — nel. Meskan.**

*— Boughs for a roof, moss under. A hall — no. A grove.*  

Word for word: *— boughs as roof, moss in(+N) low. hall — is-not. grove.*

- *Gend*, "a bough; a shoot from a stump" (reserve), plural *gendeth*; *crisur* "a roof" (reserve); *feth* "moss"; *ul vimm*, "below", the title's own *under*.
- **The wrong image corrected**, as W03's IV.4 reading corrects its own (*Tolm — nel. Nel tolm.*): *Hellur — nel.*, "a hall — it is not". Orrowen has no *no* (§3.4); the copula's negative stands for it. *Meskan*, "a grove" (O1382, "the folk of trees").
- *Gend* is also "a shoot from a stump", and the first thing Halyna name is the wood's own word for what the teller is: a cutting (ring 2).

*(second ink)*

> **— Et helv bynt. Tresketh ulo, hosen desteth orrat.**

*— The sky thin. Gaps in it, like teeth gone.*  

Word for word: *— the sky narrow. clefts in-it, as teeth gone.*

- *Et helv bynt*: the title's last words, with the article (§1.1).
- *Tresketh*, "clefts, gaps" (*tresk*, canon: "a cleft, a split; a gap"), the word in the Keep's own name (*Kethow Dhresk*); *ulo*, "in it", the preposition with its ending. *Desteth orrat*, "teeth gone" (*dest* "a tooth", reserve; *orrat* "gone"), the Book's own picture before Seren paints it whole (ring 3, *like teeth from a jaw*).

*(first ink)*

> **— Hos lyss, brid ul ryst. Nath rynn.**

*— A small one, cold at morning. Not waking.*  

Word for word: *— one small, cold in(+N) morning. NEG(+N) wake.*

- *Hos lyss*, "a small one": the numeral and the quality, and no noun, because Halyna do not yet know what it is (ring 4: a newborn).
- *Ryst* is "the morning" and "the morrow" (reserve): a small one cold in the morning, and cold on the morrow. *Brid*, "cold".
- *Nath rynn*, "does not wake" (*rynn* "wake up, rise from sleep"; the nasalising *nath* leaves *r*).

*(second ink)*

> **— Carmath hemm. Tovva. Tovva lont.**

*— Old voices. Wait. Wait with them.*  

Word for word: *— calls old. wait(IMP.PL). wait(IMP.PL) at-3PL.*

- *Carm*, "a call, a cry; a voice" (canon), plural *carmath*: the old voices.
- *Tovva*, the plural imperative of *tovv* "wait" (canon): the elders bid the many. *Tovva lont*, "wait by them": *lo* "at, by, with" with its 3pl ending, beside the pillars.

*(first ink)*

> **— Breller, domm, ul et meskrivullath. Derdeth ul et crestalath.**

*— A keel, black, in the roots. Bones in the ribs.*  

Word for word: *— keel, black, in(+N) the roots. bones in(+N) the ribs.*

- *Breller* "a keel"; *domm* "black"; *meskrivull* "a root" (O1389, "tree-foundation"), plural *meskrivullath*.
- *Derd* "a bone", *crestal* "a rib of a hull or a body" (reserve): one word for both ribs, so the Guest's bones lie in ribs that are a hull's and a body's at once.

*(second ink)*

> **— Tovva —**

*— Wait —*  

Word for word: *— wait(IMP.PL) —*

- The fourth line's word again, and broken: the old voices' side of the council, in the ink that spoke for them.

*(first ink)*

> **— nath dovvar. Nath dovva. Carmath tevel, um et hemman. Mest —**

*— no. Not wait. Young voices, over the old. Many —*  

Word for word: *— NEG(+N) N·wait-1PL. NEG(+N) N·wait(IMP.PL). calls young, upon(+S) the elders. many —*

- **Orrowen has no word for *no*:** a denial repeats the verb in the negative (§3.4). So the first ink's *no* is the young's own answer to *Tovva*, *nath dovvar*, "we wait not", and its *Not wait* is the negative imperative, *nath dovva* (as I.1's *Nath hyrrila* and S05's *nath veska dem neld*). Two inks, two sides of one council, and one verb between them.
- *Carmath tevel, um et hemman*, "young voices, over the elders": *um* "upon; across, over" (O2661); *hemman* "the elders, the old".
- *Mest —*, "many —", broken for the other ink to finish.

*(second ink)*

> **— mest sost. Nath noss kethyl gor harm lona — [ ]**

*— too many. We two cannot hold them all — [ ]*

Word for word: *— many very. NEG(+N) N·be holding every(+S) S·voice at-1DU — [ ]*

- *Mest sost*, "very many": the second ink finishing the first's word, as W04's reading finishes *Nalelv — / — eth dhaedh*.
- **"We two cannot hold them all" is the "at" idiom in the dual:** *Nath noss kethyl gor harm lona*, "the holding of every voice is not at us two" (§3.7, §3.9), with *gor harm*, "every voice" (*carm* softened after *gor*). It is the shape of Seren's own untold line, *Nath noss tul kethyl et gunn sy lo Halyna* (orrowen_v2 §11.4), in Halyna's mouth.
- The gap is the Book's `[ ]`, marked and not mended; the romanisation keeps it.

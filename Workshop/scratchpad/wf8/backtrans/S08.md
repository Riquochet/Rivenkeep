## 4 · The fixes

### 4.1 P04, the fleet's fire at noon (drift, translation)

- **Before:** *Gor fedh, lo sorenn, re yal vorr et scethan **luskat**;* "every time, at noon, the fleet's fire was loosed".
- **After:** *Gor fedh, lo sorenn, re yal vorr et scethan **ul lyssyl**;* "every time, at noon, the fleet's fire was in lessening".
- **Why this.** It is the progressive of §3.4 (*ul* + a verbal noun, as *Doss et hald ul glennol*) on *lyss* "small; reduce". The verbal noun *lyssyl* (O1337) is already in the lexicon, so no addition is needed. Read by the rules, it gives "the fleet's fire was lessening" (or "was being lessened"): either way, the fire slackened.
- **Rejected.**
  - *senn* ("go out, as a fire") says more than "slackened".
  - *omm* ("fall; fail") next to *vorr* would be heard as shells falling (*amm omm pedh*, P26).
  - The active *re lusk vorr et scethan* was already out ("fire loosed the fleet").
- **Post-fix gloss:** `every(+S) time at(+S) noon PST S·be.PST fire the fleet in(+N) reducing`.

### 4.2 P10, the fleet's new temper (wrong, translation)

- **Before:** *…eth **re hovv et scethan molt et hos ull hy ull**.* "and the fleet wore/cloaked the heart of that one after".
- **After:** *…eth **hy ull re rik et scethan molt et hos ull**.* "and after that the fleet took the heart of that one".
- **Why this.** *grik* "catch; take" (reserve, already in the leaf: *re rik o et pedh*) has no "hide" sense. *molt* stays, because it is "heart; temper" and the saying's *heart* hangs on it. *hy ull* moves to the front: after *grik* it would be heard "took … from that" (§3.6, the "from" reading after a verb of taking or going).
- **Word count:** unchanged (10 words for 10).
- **Post-fix gloss:** `and from(+S) that PST S·catch the fleet heart the one that`, which reads "the fleet took on that one's heart (temper) after that". That leads straight into *Choose which heart you leave them.*

### 4.3 P15, "I liked it less" (wrong, translation; spec gap)

- **Before:** *…eth ew gorrol velv hy ull, eth re vallom o **lyss**.* "…and I loved it little", heard as "a little".
- **After:** *…eth ew gorrol velv hy ull, eth re vallom o **lyss hy et gorrol bont**.* "…and I loved it less than the loud war".
- **Why this.** Orrowen compares with *hy* and needs the second term (lexicon §5.3, *strom hy dholm*). The term is the war before the quiet one, which came *bont* "loud" (V's *bont eth hosel eth venn*, the young wood of the first Tide). That war is the one Kael liked: "We laughed. God forgive us, we laughed."
  - This adds a term the English leaves implicit, and the unit's note says so.
  - *velv hy ull* stays: heard "quiet after that" or "quieter than that", it carries the Book's "a quieter war" either way.
- **Rejected.**
  - *lyss hy ull* (the translator's S-G2 proposal): *hy ull* is a fixed "after that; so", and it would come back "I loved it little after that".
  - *hosen amm …* ("as when …"): *hosen amm* is the lexicon's "as if".
- **Post-fix gloss:** `and PST love-1SG it small from(+S) the war loud`.

### 4.4 Not fixed, and why

- **P20, *re vaver gor sceth weskyl et clem hosel*** ("had to" came back "wanted"). *maver*'s row gives "wish, want; a need; require", so "had to" is one of its listed readings. The clean "must" idiom, *Doss maver um Y* (O0488), would put *um* before *gor*, which the grammar does not settle (pilot I.1 §3). This is a nuance of one clause in a paragraph that came back close, so it is left, and noted for the next blind reader.
- **P03, P08, P21, P30, P31** are close by design or by a word's two senses, each explained in the unit's own notes (*El nydherd en*; *Re heth o*; *dhakereth*; *Rytent ryst*; *carmath*). None is an error.

## 5 · Gaps in the spec (proposals; `orrowen_v2.md` and the shared lexicon not edited)

These are for the consolidator. Each is one line, in the place named.

1. **`orrowen_v2` §5.10 or lexicon §5.2–§5.3, "little" and "less".** A bare quality after a verb is the adverb (lexicon §5.3), and for *lyss* that is heard "a little", never "hardly". "Less", like "more", takes its term: ***lyss hy X***, "less than X". *hy ull* after a quality is heard as the fixed "after that; so", not "than that", so a comparison with no term is recast. This amends the unit's own S-G2 proposal, which S08.md now carries in its table.
2. **Lexicon row O1293, *lusk*.** "slacken" should carry a usage note: with *vorr* or *bramm* (fire, a gun), *lusk* is "loose, fire a volley", never "slacken". For a fire dying down, use *ul lyssyl* (the progressive of *lyss*) or *senn* "go out".
3. **(Minor) Lexicon row O0284, *covv*.** The verb "wear" is heard after the noun "cloak", and "cloak X" is heard as hiding X. "Take on a temper" is ***grik molt*** (as P10 now has it), not *covv molt*.

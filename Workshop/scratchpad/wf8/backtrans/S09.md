Then the main road goes; the right road goes, bound to it; the left road waits in the dread until the next step. The reach (their stone's watch), grafted, "is the ahead-road's (looks to file 0)", remembered always. Then the left road goes. In the bark: *Aelthae*.
- **E5-02, rings 1–3.** (The Lone Stroke: *one goes ahead of the rest.*) Go alone, loud and straight, as the young wood went. Stop one breath short of the fire; the hunters wait, for the fire.
- **E5-02, ring 4.** If the fire keeps silence, go as the Lone Stroke goes; if not, wait. Cut against: the stone-folk as a people, cut small (*Rhenear*): they strike first, and far; they move the guns found.
- **V7-III, rings 3–4.** When the bearer (with the carving it bears) falls, the second hull takes the carving. When the second falls, the host goes home.
- **V7-III whole.** Root: *turn the stern for home*. **Ring 2: a morrow passes.** Then the two turnings.

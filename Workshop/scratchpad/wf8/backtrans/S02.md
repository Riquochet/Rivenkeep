   - `wf7/lexicon_orrowen.md`;

   - a private copy of `orr_analyze.py`, run for its check and for `--gloss`, with a small lookup script (`lk.py`).
   
   The analyzer found 0 faults in the blind text before any reading.
3. **Frozen before the look.** The reading was saved to `wf8/tmp/S02bt/readback_draft.md` at 02:54 (sha1 `ff22014a964a…`) before `S02.md` or the Book was opened. It is reproduced unchanged in §2. ### 4.3 Spec gaps (proposed in `S02.md` Notes 8; `orrowen_v2.md` not edited, because other units read it concurrently)

- **SG1 · Indirect questions (§3.8).** *An indirect question is the question word and its clause, in the object's place (Re sesk ey ul vodh re yal o, "she knew where it was"; Hunna hosen vodh …, "hear how …"). After a bare 3sg pronoun (V o …), ul vodh is heard as the place "where", and o as "it", so there use a question word that cannot be a place (hosen vodh, amm vodh), or name the subject.* (Caused ¶2.)
- **SG2 · *dem* (§3.3, §3.6, §5.10).** *dem (+N): until, as far as; to, for (a hearer, a purpose); dem + a verbal noun is "to V, in order to V".* This brings the spec up to the lexicon's own O0390 and to how every unit uses the word. (Caused ¶20, and the "until / so that" nuance in ¶27.)
- **For the lexicon's builder.** A participle row carries only its verb's first sense: *galdat* is "built" but not "done", as *driget* was "thrown" but not "scattered" (`backtrans_I1.md` §7 item 2). S02-13 patches *galdat* only. The builder fix (`systematic()` / `en_verbs`) is still owed, and other *-At*, *-Ol* and *-Ard* rows of multi-sense verbs are likely affected.

### 4.4 Notes added to the leaf, where the text stands

- **¶20 (I.4 P3):** *dem naldol et Kethow sost* is purpose, "to build", and the blind reader's "until" is recorded against SG2.
- **¶27 (I.4 P10):** the softened *dhernil* marks *o* as "its" (§3.5), so "we never made a rite of it" is ruled out. *dem daldol …* is the purpose *dem* again.

## 6 · What was not fixed, and why

- **¶9, "I had time to be afraid."** *Re yal fedh ludalol lom* is "a time of fearing was at me". It is right, but it reads as a spell of fear, not as time *enough* for fear. That thought, the hour stretching until there is room to be afraid, then ashamed, then still, is the leaf's own. It is a nuance, so it was not changed. If the orchestrator wants it exact, the grammar offers *fedh dem ludalol*, "time for fearing". The three lines would read:
  - *Re yal fedh dem ludalol lom.*
  - *Hy ull re yal fedh dem gaemul lom um ull.* (*dem* beds *kaemul*)
  - *Hy ull re yal fedh dem hessyl lom.*
  
  With SG2 these read "I had time to fear / to be ashamed / to stop". They are not validated here because they were not adopted.
- **¶5 and ¶24: the analyzer's limit on a bedded *pa*.** *ul ba relv*, *ul ba yarl* are good grammar, and the tool reports them (the unit's Notes 4). The workarounds (*hosen pa relv hyont*, *… eth re rike pa yarl o*) cost a nuance each. The shared analyzer is not this unit's to edit. It is flagged again for whoever maintains it.
- **The gloss shows one sense.** `--gloss` prints the first row's first sense, so *galdat* still glosses "building" even with S02-13 merged. A reader has to look the word up, as this one did. Once O0657 is merged, listing "done" among its senses is enough for a lookup. Printing the participle senses in the gloss would be an analyzer improvement.
- **Polysemy the context carries:**
  - *trun* is ground or world (¶10, ¶13);
  - *hy ull* is "then" or "so" (¶11);
  - *um* is "over" or "across" (¶13);
  - *hosk* is lintel or capstone (¶8, ¶22).
  
  Each costs at most a nuance, and each sense is in the lexicon.
- **Reader error (¶27).** The text follows §3.5 exactly. Nothing to fix.

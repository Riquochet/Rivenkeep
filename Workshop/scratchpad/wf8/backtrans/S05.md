## 5 · What was not fixed, and why (for Jack, the assembler and the lexicon builder)

1. **The S5 "so … that" construction (¶16, close).** The unit writes "so X that" as *sost* + the quality, then *hy ull*. It reads right in L119 (*crisurat sost virr, hy ull …*, "roofed so close that …"). In L171 (*um et gynteth sost susk, hy ull …*) the reader took *sost* with the noun before it, and "crags so high that" came back as "the highest crags, so that". The meaning survives. But the spec has no "so X that", and the lexicon's two uses of *sost* (after its noun, "the very"; after a quality with the article, "the most") both pull the other way. **[Jack / spec owner]:** settle the order of "so X that" once, for every unit.
2. **Two lexicon gaps, for the lexicon builder** (the shared TSV is not edited in this workflow):
   - *felmyl* (O0580) should carry "sinking" as well as "drowning". This is the same verbal-noun defect as `backtrans_I1.md` §7 item 2.
   - *gannath* (O0671) should say that it is also the regular plural of *ganna* "gate", or the homophone should be flagged. S04 noted the same thing.
3. **"Loud all day" (¶12, close).** *Gor orl* is "every day". If the assembler wants the exact nuance, the unit's own pattern gives *lo dhrenn et orl*, "through the day". Left as it is: it is a nuance, not an error.
4. **Differences built in on purpose** (¶7, 26, 27, 35, 43) were scored close and left alone. Each is declared in the unit's §6 table.
5. **The reader's parses** (¶34 "first walls", ¶41 "snow/white", ¶26 "one another") each cost at most a nuance, and the Orrowen is correct in each.

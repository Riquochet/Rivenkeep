**The Orrowen frame is sound.** Its two "close" sentences are choices, not errors: the translator's explicit "for" in H2, and the polysemy of *haral* ("an order; a command; a law") in H3, which I read as "order" where the Book has "law". Nothing in the frame was changed; the analyzer still reads all 147 words OK.

**The grain had six errors, and one pattern accounts for three of the six drifting or wrong rings.**
1. **"Went out of us" was cut as our going (rings 4, 5, 6).** An act stands on its doer's file (§10.3), so `12: GO` reads "*we* went". Ring 6, where the dread goes out of the hull to every hull of the fleet, came back as the hull itself sailing into the host. That ring is **wrong**. The fix is our sending, `12: >GO`, with `→` the knowing and `→in` where it went.
2. **"The guns let us go" was `GUN×3+>GO?` (ring 3).** It came back as "did the guns fire after us?", the reverse. `>` is "make go; send". The fix uses A15's *let go*, `!HOLD →`.
3. **"The sea came in" (ring 2) and "with the sea in us already" (ring 8) were carried by a WATER band** on our own file. A band is a surrounding, so both came back only as "on the water". The fix cuts the sea as a doer, `4: TIDE  4: GO →in` the wound, with a ray joining it to ring 8.
4. **The bearer's heart lost the bearer (rings 6–7).** A plain `HEART` standing first on file 14 becomes the file's referent by §11.2, because A16 does not list the heart as a belonging. The fix is `BEARER+HEART`, a new compound.
5. **Two files lost their referents by rule (ring 8).**
   - `FLASH{still}` stood first on file 0, which made "the flash" the wall's file for rings 8 and 10. The stone is now cut first.
   - The surf stood on file 3, where ring 5's hollow shore-man is still the referent. It is now on file 15.
6. **"The hand … is new to each of them" was `0: NEW` (ring 10).** By rule, that says *you* (the wall) are new, against the knowing just before it. The fix is `HAND+NEW`.

Spec-level causes and proposals are findings 6–12 of `wf8/additions/W05_grain.md`.

### 2.1 Seren's frame (Orrowen, ink hand)

- **Title.** *Kaelir sa re vymm dem varn*: "The wreck that came back home."
- **H1.** "As the Mystwood remembers: known from one hull of the Tide of Remembering, which sank on its second coming. It turned back from our wall, hurt, and came back to the very same stone, to die there. It faces the Night of the Naming."
- **H2.** "Halvard held it (knew it), and his hands were on it. Rhyna did not touch it that day, because salt-cuts were open in her palms; but she knew it as he knew it."
- **H3.** "The hull's own telling was laid down quickly. Halyna weighed the order at its end for seven nights, and only then did they let me write it."
  - Uncertain: *haral* is "an order; a command; a law; to bid", so "the command at its end" is the literal sense.
- **H4.** "Told by Halyna, by turns, in two inks; set down by Seren." (formula)
- **H5.** "Where the wood was broken, or the telling failed, it is marked and not mended." (formula)
- **Close.** "And no one answered." (formula)
- The leaf-hand block transliterates the same lines letter for letter and adds nothing to the sense.

### 2.2 The wood's telling (the grain)

*Seal:* This is held in the grain. Root STERN: the telling is *of* going home when wounded. File 12, the teller, first holds HULL: we are a hull.

1. We were a hull of the Tide of Remembering (*Ralenthae*), which the roots taught. More than a word was cut in us:
   - so that we would remember (HOLD with the memory ray);
   - and so that we would watch (EYE: the heart's plain stand-in for "a caution").

   In the grey, at morning, we went with our kin and the bearer going ahead, to the wall.
2. A stone flashed; and where a stone flashes, then a gun. This the young wood learned, and the roots carried it home. We turned aside toward the stone. The stone spoke again, and then the iron went into us, through a low wound under the water-line, on the water.
3. Then an older carving woke in us, the first of the Turned Stern's: *Home when wounded*. We turned our stern to the wall, and went low and slowly back into the grey, toward home. Did the guns fire after us as we went, or did they not see us? (Either, and we do not know which.)
4. Still going, we spoke to our kin as we went, for wood has no need of words among its kin. We went on with the stone and its flash, into the roots.
5. We came back to our kin with our wound, and they held our wound in their own wood. It was as shore-men would feel each other's wounds, by turns, each to the other, across a city; but the shore-men are only a likeness.
6. We went on through the dread, with only dying wood on the water beside us (at our right). Slowly, as sap moves, we went to all the host; and as fast as we could we went to its heart, the heart of the whole host.
7. The stone was mended. The heart of the host carved a caution for the next coming: *look, then trust*. A stone that has spoken and then does not speak: is it silent? How long does it wait? In the next coming we went out in front, because we had gone first before, to watch whether the stone still flashed. The heart sent only us, to look.
8. The stone still flashed. The second time we did not turn aside, there on the water. The iron found us and struck us again, as though it had kept count. We went down in the lee of the stone. We lay in the white, and our kin went over us and on past. The tide moved us as we lay, and the breakers carried us onto the shingle. Many hands came to us.
9. We spoke, and then we fell. Our kin, living in the grey, carried it; then the roots carried it home, to the groves, where the next hulls are growing, and they grow holding it.
10. Know this rightly. The groves learned the stone:
    - that stones hide guns;
    - that a silent stone is not a spent one;
    - that when a hull turns and runs, the iron follows.

    But they did not learn the hand: whether it will send its guns, or how long it will wait. Every hull that watched for the hand, its sending and its waiting, fell and did not come home. They went to the wall knowing the stone; to all of them the hand was new.
11. Once we went home, and spoke; then we went out again to the wall, to watch, and died watching. The groves hold it.

*Seal:* …and the grain holds it still.

**Points marked uncertain in the draft.** Six of them became findings below.
1. **File 0 from ring 8.** By §11.2 it is "the flash", and the validator reads it that way. I read it as the wall, by sense.
2. **File 3 in ring 8.** By rule it is still the hollow shore-man; I read it as the surf.
3. **Ring 8's bands** spread over all four of our marks in the packed year.
4. **`HAND+!OPEN`** could be "no need" or "keeps nothing back".
5. **`HEART` on file 14** I read as the host's heart.
6. **`TRUE+HOLD` on file 0** reads "believes" by rule. I read it as an injunction only because of the register row.
7. **`GO#1`**: "once" or "a first time".

## 1 · Method, and how blind it was

1. **Mapping without reading.** I mapped `units/W02.md` by line prefix, heading and fence only:
   - an `awk` count of each line's leading token;
   - `grep '^#'` for the headings, with the `# file` legend lines filtered out of the output;
   - the fence positions.

   I then checked the code blocks for English stop-words, by count only.
2. **The blind copy.** A script (`tmp/W02bt/extract.py`) copied the native lines into `wf8/blind/W02.txt`: 1,233 lines, sha1 `dcf5cf3c…`, 03:06:20. It holds:
   - the 10 blockquoted Orrowen lines (§1), labelled T, H1–H8 and C;
   - the 10-line leaf-hand token block (§1.4);
   - the knowing-form GN of §2 (band I once, each heart's ring 7 with its bark, ring 8);
   - the ten canonical rounds of §4.

   Two things were stripped from the copy:
   - every `#` comment line, which removed the 230 `# file` legend lines;
   - a trailing parenthesis on a code line, which removed two English annotations.

   Only the heart names were kept from the `####` headings, and the round ids carry them anyway.
3. **The reading.** It used only these sources:
   - `grain_v2.md` in full, with `W02_grain.md` appended in a private copy (`tmp/W02bt/root/wf7/`);
   - `orrowen_v2.md` §0–§3 and §5.10–§5.12;
   - `lexicon_orrowen.md` §4–§8;
   - the private merged lexicon (`tmp/W02bt/lexicon.tsv`: 2,880 shared rows + 10 W02 rows);
   - `orr_analyze.py --gloss` (with its set-phrase hints), `tmp/W02bt/gloss.txt`, and a row lookup (`lk.py`);
   - the validator's plain and `--literal` readings of the comment-free canonical GN (`tmp/W02bt/read/`).

   `tokcheck.py` confirmed that the leaf-hand tokens spell the romanised lines, bites and harmonic letters included (10 of 10). I did not open `coverage/concepts.tsv` or `grain_coverage.md`, nor the cited Judgement rounds.
4. **Frozen before the look.** The whole reading was written to `tmp/W02bt/blind_draft.md` and made read-only (sha1 `574be7a6…`, 03:15:02). Only then was `units/W02.md` read (a backup copy was taken at 03:15:16, unread). §2 reproduces the draft.

## 3 · The comparison, paragraph by paragraph

- **Exact:** every proposition and relation comes back; only the wording differs.
- **Close:** every main proposition comes back; a nuance, image or modality shifts.
- **Drift:** a proposition is lost or changed.
- **Wrong:** a proposition is contradicted or invented.

A drifting paragraph that holds one invented clause is scored drift, with the clause named, as `backtrans_IV4.md` scored its ring 5.

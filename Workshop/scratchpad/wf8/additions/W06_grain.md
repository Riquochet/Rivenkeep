# W06 · V.4 The Council Under the Thin Sky · grain additions (proposed; not yet in grain_v2.md)

*wf8 unit W06. Compounds that V.4's whole carving uses. Each is modifier + head, as Seilrhass builds (mystaeri_spec §2.5); its soft reading is the compound of the two signs' soft readings (computed, not coined; call §19.8; the plural in -en as `seilen`, `neivathen`). No new sign and no new rule of the notation was needed. Without these glosses the validator reads the ligatures part by part ("seems to break and hold", "live and go", "fell with iron and stop", "an own moss", "our opening many hands", "many striking and carve"), which is legal but misleading. Two rows (`OVER+HULL×3`, `BENEATH+SIT`) are the same compounds W01 and W02 propose, with the same glosses, repeated here only so this unit's private merge reads them. To be merged into grain_v2.md §21 (a new §21.x "Compounds added by V.4") by whoever owns the shared spec.*

## Findings (for the spec's owner; none blocks)

1. **§7.1's V.4 example should follow B12.** §7.1 says that "the vote was carried by the weight of anger and grief" is the young's runner breaking the elders' plea. B12 moved the break inside ring 12, and the leaf cuts it as B12 says. The council's choosing, run to war (`w3`), breaks the elders' plea for patience, which is cut again in ring 12 (`w4`). The young's anger and grief run to the choosing, braided `ab`. So the runner that breaks is the council's, carried by the young's two strands, and §7.1's wording could say so.
2. **A band in a one-cell ring covers exactly one mark.** Rings 2 and 3 have one cell to a slot by §4.4: their girth is too small for two. Every mark on the sky's file is therefore a year of its own, and "thin", "the breath ran out" and "the between-light" each need their own `band MIST` line. This is the IV.4 pilot's finding (a band's year is its knowing's first mark) seen from the other side. No change is asked, but it is worth a line in A8.
3. **The proof rings' files.** `coverage/tests/V-4_proof.gn2` put the elders on file 6, an even file (a bearing), and the silver elders on file 10. §11.2 wants persons on odd files, and IV.6 keeps the council-grove behind, on 8 (home). The leaf puts the elders on 7 and the grove on 8. It also makes the council, not the young, the doer of ring 13's taking and cutting, since the vote was the council's.
4. **The validator's pocket reading shows only the first shoot of a split runner.** `P1.2 → {P1.3, P1.4, P1.7}` read only "pillars → not grow". The leaf writes its pocket runners singly (`d4`, `d4b`, `d4c` …), which also reads more truly: each is its own doing.
5. **Verb-first glosses.** The validator glosses a lone MOUTH as a verb ("seem to speak" for the hollow jaw of ring 2) and a lone THIN as "thin one (thin wood)" (the sky's thinness). The reading is only a gloss (§13.5), but a noun-first gloss for a lone mark that governs nothing would help a blind decoder.
6. **§13.4 does not order rays among themselves.** This leaf writes its four rays by file.
7. **Comings.** V.4 has four: the Aelvaren home, the Guest home in ashes, the young into the grove, the iron to the edge. All are `GO → @f` or `→in @f` by §9.2, and they read well; the missing sign for *come* was not felt.
8. **The register's jar and strength** (`~BREAK+HOLD`, `LIVE`) were the only register readings V.4 needed that the validator could not gloss. Both are compounds above, not new A15 rows.
9. **A16 names no feeling as a belonging** (found by the blind back-translation, `wf8/backtrans/W06.md`). A16 lists a person's mind, word, name, breath, hand, eye, bones and blood, but not the heart and its feelings: `HEART`, `ANGER`, `SHAME`, `GLAD` and `GRIEF`. So a lone `ANGER` cut first on the young's file names *anger* as that file's referent, and a blind reader loses the young. The validator agrees ("5 [anger (heat)]") once the `# file` tags are taken away. The leaf no longer relies on it: ring 11 now cuts `ANGER+SAPLING×3`, the angry young. Ring 12's `GREAT+ANGER` and `GRIEF` still name "a great anger" and "grief", which is what the Book says there ("the weight of anger and grief"). **Proposed for A16:** a person's heart and feelings are belongings of the person standing on the file, as the mind is.
10. **A16 names no edge of a place** (the same back-translation). `EDGE` cut first on the grove's file in ring 13 ("at the edge of the grove") names *the edge* as file 8's referent. Ring 14's "The grove had not waited" (`8: !KNOT`) then reads "the edge did not wait". The leaf now adds `ray 8: r1/1–r14/1`: the one that did not wait is the grove of ring 1 (v1's ray, "the same one"). **Proposed for A16:** an `EDGE` cut on the file of a place already standing there is that place's edge, as `DOOR` and `SQUARE` are a built place's. Ring 2's `EDGE{all}`, which stands first on an empty bearing, would still name the edge of the wood.

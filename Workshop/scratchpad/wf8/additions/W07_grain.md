**Two validator notes** (for `grain_validate.py`, none blocks anything).

- **A thing that is also a count reads as a verb.** `LIVE#1`, the A9 substitute for "one life" (V.6 ring 2), reads "living once": a count on an act is "n times" (v1 §3.7). The substitute is the spec's own (A9), and the proof ring used it; a reader should be told that in A9's substitute the `#1` is "one", not "once". Proposed: say so in A9's table, or let the reading treat `LIVE#1` that two *with* runners reach as "one life".
- **`X+HAND` is five.** A ligature whose head is HAND reads "five of X" (v1 §3.7), so "angry hands" cannot be `ANGER+HAND×3`, and "the wood's hands" cannot be `WOOD+HAND×3`. V.6 cuts `HAND×3+ANGER` (hands, in anger: the hand as modifier) and "for they are the wood's hands" as the wood's knowing *with* their hands (`HOLD →with HAND`). The five-rule is right; the register should warn against the trap (the concept register has no row for "angry hands").

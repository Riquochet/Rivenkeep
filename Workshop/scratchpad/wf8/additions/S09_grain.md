## 2 · Amendment: *Rhenear* cut small, in E5-02's aim

V.7 II says: "There was a name for us cut small in that carving … It was *Rhenear*: the stone-deaf." The canon's own table (mystaeri_spec §3.9) reads "*Rhenear* = STONEFOLK×3½". Its device table (§3.7, half size) says that the canon uses half size once: *Rhenear*, "cut small" (V.7 II). The Deceit Tide's first-appearance row puts it at the aim place: "the aim place (band III, file 8) with STONEFOLK; *Rhenear* cut half size". The coverage text of E5-02 has no half-size mark. It cuts the doer of the habits whole, twice, inside two ligatures.

| | Ring 4, file 8 (the aim) |
|---|---|
| coverage text | `8.1: STONEFOLK×3+STRIKE@1    8.2: DEEP+STRIKE    8.3: STONEFOLK×3+>GO` |
| **S09** | `8.1: STONEFOLK×3‿½    8.2: DEEP+STRIKE@1    8.3: >GO` |

- *Rhenear* stands once, alone, cut small, with the kind-arc of grain_v2 §5.3 ("`STONEFOLK×3‿` is *Rhenear* or *Aethear*"; §19 item 9 recommends redrawing *Rhenear* with it). The canonical suffix order is `×3 ‿ ½` (A7).
- The habits then stand on its file as acts. The file is the pronoun (§10.11), so *they* is not cut again. "Strike first and far" is `DEEP+STRIKE@1`: DEEP is *eir*, deep, long and far, as in `DEEP+ROAD` for the far road. "Move the guns we find" is `>GO` (make go) with its runner to `FIND+GUN×3` on file 10, as before.
- The change removes two signs, and runner t5 is unchanged. Nothing else in E5-02 moves.
- **For the builder.** The Book of Knowings' own round of E5-02 should take the same ring 4. V.7 II is the canon's only statement of what that aim holds.

## 4 · Proposal for Jack: the Last Tide carving's root, and its elided sap

- **The root.** The Book does not say which line the V.7 III plan belongs to. An order must cut a line root in its heart (§10.1, v1 G1). **Proposed: `STERN`, the Turned Stern.** Its green-wood form is E1-07, *Home when wounded*. So the round's heart would say the young wood's *home*, "the place a hurt thing runs to", and its last ring would say the new *home*, the place wanted. Seren's reflection in V.7 III ("The young wood had known *home* only as the place a hurt thing runs to …") is then visible in one round, heart to bark. That is the Law of the Rings: "the eldest carvings are the youngest ones grown old". The alternative is `BARB`, the Bar Before. It is the line of the bearer ("Before the bearer", "Screen the bearer"), and its Judgement, Leavaren's *Strike one head; two will answer*, is also about succession. **[Jack]**
- **The sap is not told.** "A whole morrow carved into its heart, a plan with turnings in it … the longest knowing Halyna had ever held": the Book gives only its end. The GN writes the heart ring and the two turnings of band III. Band II is **elided**, and `r2 ·` stands in for it only so that the round parses. By v1 §3.7 an empty ring reads "a morrow passes", which is not a claim this file makes. The ring numbers of band III are therefore those of the stand-in, not of the true round. The Book's inline plate should show the two turnings only. The stand-in line now carries a trailing comment, `r2 ·  (untold: …)`, and the notation this needs is proposed in §6.
- **No name in the bark.** V.7 III does not say that the heart held a name. *Eirthae*, `DEEP+TIDE`, is left out.

## 6 · From the blind back-translation: the untold ring (a spec gap, proposed rule) **[Jack]**

*Added by the blind back-translation of this unit, `wf8/backtrans/S09.md`.*

- **What came back.** The comment-free V7-III was read by rule, and its `r2 ·` came back as "a morrow passes". The validator reads it the same way ("Ring 2: a morrow passes."). So the GN, read by rule, adds a proposition the Book does not make. The Book says the Standard-Bearer carried "a whole morrow carved into its heart, a plan with turnings in it": the sap is **full but untold**, not empty. v1 §3.1 ("Truth or nothing") and §3.7 (an empty ring is *a morrow passes*) leave no honest way to write a ring that the wood holds and the Book does not tell.
- **Nothing honest parses today.** Two variants were tried:
  - dropping band II (`rules after: [1, 1]`, with an empty `II`) fails with S04, "band-rules must stand in order";
  - dropping the `II` line (`rules after: [1]`) fails with S05, because band III's rings then stand in band II.

  Both variants also raise the validator's warning A16 (a long life grows 4–9 rings).
- **What the unit does now.** The stand-in stays, and it says what it is on its own line: `r2 ·  (untold: the plan's sap is not in the Book; a stand-in, not a morrow passing)`. A trailing `(…)` after two spaces is a comment to the validator (as in the v1 samples), and it still reads the round with 0 errors and 0 warnings. Unlike a `#` line, the comment travels with the ring line, so a copy of the rings keeps it. The round is a validation scaffold: it is never drawn whole, and the Book's plate shows the two turnings only (§4).
- **Proposed rule (for the next spec pass, to be numbered there among §21's Additions; it is not in `grain_v2.md`, which this unit may not edit).** The **untold ring** is written `rK …` (an ellipsis where the empty ring has its dot). It means "the wood holds a knowing here that the telling does not give". It is never drawn: a round with an untold ring is shown only as the plates of its told rings. It is never read as *a morrow passes*. It counts toward `rings:` and the band-rules as an empty ring does, and it takes no marks, runners or rays. It serves every carving the Book cites only in part: this Last Tide plan, and the parent rings that E5-02's coverage text elides silently.
- **For the validator.** It should accept `rK …`, and read it "(not told)". Until then it reads any stand-in as "a morrow passes", even with the trailing comment.

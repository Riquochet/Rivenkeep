### 1.2 The headnote

> **Hosen tum Mesk Myst: kethet hy volt vovennerd Hyll Lodhat, tevet ul et meskanath hosast sa re fymm et redul; doss o ul lern Hylleth sa re nydh et Hald.**

as · remember · Mystwood: held (known) · from · S·heart · S·Standard-Bearer · Tide · Bonded, grown · in(+N) · the · grove-PL · first · REL(+S) · PST(+S) · sow · the · council; is · it · in(+N) · face · Tide-PL · REL(+S) · PST(+S) · count · the · Wall.

*As the Mystwood remembers: known from the heart of a Standard-Bearer of the Joined Tide, grown in the first groves the council sowed; it faces the Tides, as the Wall Counted Them.*

- *Hosen tum Mesk Myst: kethet hy …* and *doss o ul lern …* are IV.4's formulas exactly. ***hy volt vovennerd Hyll Lodhat*** is W04's chain of constructs (*hy volt vovennerd Hyll Dhumol*) with the Joined Tide, *Hyll Lodhat* (O1047, "the Tide Bonded"); *h* does not soften.
- **"Their council set" is *sa re fymm et redul*, "that the council sowed"**: *fymm* "sow seed" (reserve), because the leaf itself insists the wood is grown, not built, so Seren does not write *gald* "build, raise". "Their" is the article: *so redul* would set *so* before *r*, a letter the softening leaves unchanged, and nothing would mark the possessive (§3.5); the council is theirs by the sentence.
- *Hylleth sa re nydh et Hald* is V.5's title (O1054).

> **Re lymm Halvard o dem susk hy et dhysal um et sest, eth amm re yal o umo tul, re rarr o ulonta pana, Rhyna lo et odh.**

PST · bring · Halvard · it · to(+N) · height · from · the · shingle · upon(+S) · the · shoulder, and · when · PST · S·be · it · upon-him · yet, PST · S·come · it · in-them-two · both, Rhyna · at · the · hearth.

*Halvard carried it up from the shingle on his shoulder, and while it was still upon him it came into them both, Rhyna at the hearth.*

- *lymm* "bring; bear, carry" (reserve); *dem susk* "up, upward" (O0394); *hy et dhysal*, "from the shingle" (O0458).
- **"On his shoulder" is *um et sest*, "on the shoulder"**: *um o sest* would set *o* before *s*, which does not soften, and read "upon it, shoulder" (§3.5). The article serves, as for a body's part.
- **"Before he set it down" is "while it was still upon him"**, *amm re yal o umo tul*: Orrowen has no conjunction "before" (*ul lern* is "in front of", a place; *tevar* "early"), and the knowing came while he still carried it.
- ***re rarr o ulonta pana***, "it came into them two, both": IV.4's *re rarr o uloy niss* ("it came into her slowly") with the dual, *ulonta* "in them two", and *pana* "both".

> **Veth ew strom o hyonta, eth re sol o ul et nelter, somm nath re yal tul kethyl scetheth mest olen lo Halyna.**

but · was · great · it · than-them-two, and · PST · rest · it · in(+N) · the · vault, while · NEG · PST · S·be · yet · holding · hull-PL · many · together · at · Halyna.

*But it was too great for them, and it lay in the vault while Halyna could not yet hold many hulls at once.*

- **"Too great for them" is "greater than they two"**: *hy* is also "than" (§3.6, *hos strom hyo*), conjugated in the dual, *hyonta*. "Then" is left to the tenses and to *tul*: *amm ull* ("then") beside *hyonta* is read by the analyzer's hints as *hy amm*, "since".
- ***somm nath re yal tul kethyl … lo Halyna* is Seren's own untold-leaf line in the past** (orrowen_v2 §11.4, *Nath noss tul kethyl et gunn sy lo Halyna*, "Halyna cannot yet hold this deep"), with the negative past *nath re* (O2861). "Until they had learned" is "while they could not yet": *dem* "until" takes a noun or a verbal noun (*dem sennyl*), not a clause, and before *amm* it would bed the vowel. Halvard and Rhyna are the pair, and are named by the pair-name, with its lintel.
- *sol* "rest, lie still" (canon) for "lay"; *olen* "together" for "at once" (*hos fedh* is "once, one time"); *scetheth*, the slender plural of *sceth*.

> **Ew sell brodhol et mesk sy hy gor wrodhol tevar.**

was · long · telling · the · wood · this · than · every(+S) · S·telling · early.

*No wood before it took so long to tell* (literally, *the telling of this wood was longer than every telling before*).

- The copula with the predicate first, as IV.4's *Ew duss sost brodhol et trenn*; *hy* for "than" again; *gor* softens *brodhol* to *wrodhol*; *tevar* "early, before the time" (reserve).
- The shorter *brodhol vesk*, "a wood's telling", is avoided: the possessor *mesk* softened is *vesk*, and *vesk* is "see" (the analyzer reads it so).

> **Re wrodha Halyna o, hos eth ullen, ul ba luth; re hadh Seren o.**

PST · S·tell-3DU · Halyna · it, one · and · other, in · N·two · ink; PST · S·lay · Seren · it.

*Told by Halyna, by turns, in two inks; set down by Seren.*

- IV.4's formula exactly (O2858 *hos eth ullen*). *Halyna* carries its sealing lintel in the leaf-hand; *Seren* has none ("one name is a mourning").

> **Amm re yal et mesk cresket, ell re omm et brodhol, doss o rellorat eth nahlennet; eth re hadhom Vell et Surrath, ul o rask, ul vell, virr hosen lusk et luth.**

when · PST · S·be · the · wood · broken, or · PST · fail · the · telling, is · it · marked · and · unmended; and · PST · S·lay-1SG · Song · the · Years, in · its · S·end, in(+N) · verse, near · as · allows · the · ink.

*Where the wood was broken, or the telling failed, it is marked, and not mended; and the Song of the Years at its end I have set as verse, as near as ink allows.*

- The first clause is IV.4's formula word for word; Seren's addition follows its semicolon, as the Book's does.
- ***re hadhom***, "I laid": *cadh* "lay" is the verb Seren sets a telling down with (*re hadh Seren o*), here in her own first person, softened after *re*. *Vell et Surrath* is the lexicon's name for the Song (O2700); *ul o rask*, "in its end", is IV.4's.
- **"As verse" is *ul vell*, "in song"**: *vell* is "a song; a rhyme; verse", so the Song of the Years is laid in song. "As near as ink allows" is *virr hosen lusk et luth*, "near, as the ink allows" (*lusk* "allow, let").

## 3 · WHAT THE TRANSLATION DID, AND WHY

### 3.1 The grain

1. **Every sentence is carved, once** (§10.11). One mark to a content word; a referent already cut in a knowing is pointed at, never cut again; a clause shared by two knowings is carved in the first (ring 21's *doom* and *We were sent* are one runner). **Files are the pronouns**: *we* is file 12, *you* file 0, and nine grains take nine files in ring 8. ### 3.3 Orrowen

- **No new word.** Every word of the frame is in the lexicon; W07's Orrowen additions file holds the header row only.
- **The formulas are IV.4's, word for word**: *Hosen tum Mesk Myst: kethet hy …*, *doss o ul lern …*, *Re wrodha Halyna o, hos eth ullen, ul ba luth; re hadh Seren o.*, *Amm re yal et mesk cresket, ell re omm et brodhol, doss o rellorat eth nahlennet*, and *Eth re dhess nahos.* The Standard-Bearer's heart is W04's construct chain with the Joined Tide.
- **Seren echoes herself.** "It lay in the vault until they had learned to hold many hulls at once" is her untold-leaf note (orrowen_v2 §11.4) set in the past: *somm nath re yal tul kethyl scetheth mest olen lo Halyna*, "while Halyna could not yet hold many hulls together". It needs the negative past *nath re* (O2861, a proposal of the lexicon's, lexicon_orrowen.md §8.3); without it, the line would be *somm nath noss tul …* in the present, as her note has it.
- **Three recasts for the possessive that cannot show** (§3.5): *et redul* for "their council" (*so redul*), *um et sest* for "on his shoulder" (*um o sest*), and the object pronoun *o* stands only before a vowel, a letter the softening leaves unchanged, or a preposition (*o dem susk*), so it is never read as "its".
- **Two words kept out of the frame on purpose**: *brodhol vesk* ("a wood's telling", heard as *vesk* "see") and *amm ull* beside *hyonta* (read as *hy amm*, "since").
- **"Before" is recast as "while … still"**, and "until" as "while … not yet": the lexicon has no temporal *before*, and *dem* "until" takes a noun, not a clause.

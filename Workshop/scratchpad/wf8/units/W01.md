### 1.2 The headnote

> **Hosen tum Mesk Myst: kethet hy hebbet et arra sa re lumm, lo hethyl gunn sost, ul Hyll sa re rystull nycul; doss o ul lern dhrenn et Kethow, hy vodh el trenneth varn pana.**

as · remember · Mystwood: held (known) · from · gift · the · guest · REL · PST · kneel, at · S·holding · deep · very, in(+N) · Tide · REL · PST · learn · deceit; is · it · in(+N) · face · S·tale · the · Keep, from · what · is · tales · home · both.

*As the Mystwood remembers: known from the gift of the Kneeling Guest at its deepest holding, in the Tide that Learned Deceit, and set facing the tale of the Keep, for both are tales of home.*

- ***Hosen tum Mesk Myst: kethet hy …*** is the wood leaves' opening, exactly as IV.4 writes it; *kethet*, "held", is the canon's verb for knowing wood, never *sesket*.
- ***et arra sa re lumm***, "the guest who knelt", is the Book's "the Kneeling Guest", built as the canon builds an epithet with the relative (*Voss sa re vess*, "Voss who asked", §3.10), on IV.4's *et arra*, "the Guest". It is in the past because he knelt once. The stone text needs the name again (IV.5, "the Kneeling Guest's rite"; the names table), so it is added as a set phrase (§5.1). *hebbet et arra …* is a construct: the head takes no article, the possessor keeps its own.
- ***lo hethyl gunn sost***, "at the very deep holding": *kethyl* softened after *lo*; *gunn sost*, "deepest" (*sost* for the most, lexicon §5.3), the word of Seren's own untold-leaf note (*kethyl et gunn sy*, "the holding of this deep").
- ***doss o ul lern dhrenn et Kethow***, "it faces the tale of the Keep", is IV.4's facing formula (*doss o ul lern Yebb et Pard*), with *trenn* softened as the possessor of *lern*. A participle, *doss o cadhat ul lern …* ("it is set facing"), was tried and dropped: after *o* an unmutated word is heard as "its …" (§3.5), and the analyzer asks for *hadhat*.
- ***hy vodh el trenneth varn pana***, "for tales of home are both": the copula puts the predicate first (*El tolm et hald*). *trenneth* is the plural of *trenn* with a sense of its own, "the tales of a night": the hearth's tales are laid one a night.

> **Re hethe Halyna o, hos eth ullen, lo dhrenn vesseth mest: hos lo et odh ul o hethyl, somm re aedh ullen; eth re rynn et aedherd ul gethyl et galat sa re heth ullen.**

PST · S·hold-3DU · Halyna · it, one · and · other, at · S·course · nights · many: one · at · the · hearth · in(+N) · its · S·holding, while · PST · sleep · other; and · PST · wake · the · sleeper · in(+N) · N·holding · the · thing · REL · PST · S·hold · other.

*Halyna held it by turns through many nights, one at the hearth with it while the other slept, and the sleeper woke knowing what the other had held.*

- **Halyna are two, and take the dual**: *kethe* (*keth* + 3DU), softened after *re*, as §3.7's own example, *Re hethe Halyna et mesk*. Their pair-name carries its sealing lintel in the leaf-hand.
- ***hos eth ullen***, "by turns", is IV.4's phrase (O2858). The Book says "by turns" twice in this headnote, and so does Seren.
- ***lo dhrenn vesseth mest***, "by the course of many nights": *lo dhrenn*, "along, through" (lexicon §5.1), lays the nights as a course is laid; *mest*, "many".
- ***ul o hethyl***, "holding it": the progressive (*ul* + a verbal noun, §3.4), with the pronoun object written as the verbal noun's possessor, as IV.4's *maver o wrodhol*; *ul o* stays unmutated, as IV.4's *ul o rask*.
- ***eth re rynn et aedherd ul gethyl et galat sa re heth ullen***, "the sleeper woke holding the thing the other had held": *rynn*, "wake up, rise from sleep"; *aedherd*, "a sleeper" (*aedh* + -Ard); *galat sa …*, "the thing that …", as I.1's *galat sa nath re nydhym*. "Knowing" is *keth* again, because it is the wood that is known.

> **Ew duss sost o ul so wrodholath, hy vodh nath noss brodath lo Orrow um varn sa el hoss; eth nath re lusk hos brod ulo, voll, o wrodhol fedh.**

was · hard · very · it · in(+N) · their · S·tellings, from · what · NEG · N·be · words · at · Shore · upon · home · REL · is · breath; and · NEG · PST · allow · one · word · in-it, name, its · S·telling · ever.

*It was the hardest of their tellings, for the shore has no words for a home that is air; and one word in it, a name, would not be told at all.*

- ***Ew duss sost …*** is IV.4's "hardest" (*Ew duss sost brodhol et trenn …*), here "among their tellings": *so* softens *brodholath* to *wrodholath*.
- ***nath noss brodath lo Orrow***, "the Shore has no words": the "at" idiom for having, negated (§3.7).
- ***um varn sa el hoss***, "about a home that is breath": *um* is "upon; about, concerning". The Book's "air" is ***hoss***, "breath", the word of the leaf's own title. Orrowen has the sky (*helv*) and the breath, and no third word for air; the wood's air is its breath.
- ***nath re lusk hos brod ulo, voll, o wrodhol fedh***, "one word in it, a name, would not allow its telling, ever". The Book's "would not be told" is a refusal, the word's own, so it is *lusk*, "let, allow", Kael's verb in I.1 (*Nath re lusk o lom kylyl wrod hyo*, "he would not let me change a word of it"). *nath re* is the negative past (O2861); *ulo*, "in it", is the conjugated preposition; *fedh*, "ever", after the negative, as I.1's *re hyrril nahos fedh*.

> **Re wrodha Halyna o, hos eth ullen, ul ba luth; re hadh Seren o.**

PST · S·tell-3DU · Halyna · it, one · and · other, in · N·two · ink; PST · S·lay · Seren · it.

*Told by Halyna, by turns, in two inks; set down by Seren.*

- IV.4's formula, exactly. *Halyna* carries its sealing lintel; *Seren* has none ("one name is a mourning").

> **Amm re yal et mesk cresket, ell re omm et brodhol, doss o rellorat eth nahlennet.**

when · PST · S·be · the · wood · broken, or · PST · fail · the · telling, is · it · marked · and · unmended.

*Where the wood was broken, or the telling failed, it is marked, and not mended.*

- IV.4's formula, exactly. In II.2 the promise is kept at once: the name the telling failed to tell is marked (the chip, §1.3), and not mended.

### 1.3 Seren's note on the name (the ripening chip)

The Book sets the name the wood gives itself as a chip (the NAELEAR sign) in ring 1, with a `::: note` that ripens when VI.1 is laid. The note's two labels ("The name the wood gives itself, not yet told"; "Translation — unlocked by …") are the game's surface; its gloss is in Seren's voice. Both lines of hers are given in her ink; the unlock line is left to the builder.

> **Et voll sa voll et mesk o sost; nawrodhat tul.**

the · name · REL · name · the · wood · it · self; untold · yet.

*The name the wood gives itself, not yet told.*

> **Naelear, et hossen. Cadh Seren et brod lo sest et rellor, eth ston et rellor.**

Naelear, the · of-breath. lay · Seren · the · word · at · shoulder · the · sign, and · stand · the · sign.

*Naelear, those of the breath. Seren sets the word beside the sign, and the sign stays.*

- *voll* is noun and verb ("a name; to name"); *nawrodhat*, "untold", is Seren's canon word (§11.4), and *tul*, "yet".
- ***et hossen***, "the ones of breath": the adjective *hossen*, "alive; of breath", taken as a noun with the article, as *et strom sost*, "the greatest". It is Seren's gloss of *Naelear*, as *mystan* is her gloss of *Mystaeri*. ***hossan*** (*hoss* + -an, "the folk of", on the model of *mystan*) was tried and dropped: *hoss* is also a verb ("breathe"), and *hossan* is its first dual, "we two breathe", which is how the analyzer reads it.
- ***eth ston et rellor***, "and the sign stands": *ston* is the word that closes every oath. The sign stays as a vow stays.

## 3 · WHAT THE TRANSLATION DID, AND WHY

**The grain.**

1. **Every sentence is carved, once** (§10.11). One mark to a content word; a referent already cut is pointed at, never cut again: the sun's flash is reached by two *because* runners in ring 4, the anchors by our naming in ring 9, the hidden "us" by three runners in ring 13. **Files are the pronouns**: *we* is file 12, *you* file 0, the grey file 11, and "whoever" is simply file 13 with no thing-sign on it.
2. **One file plan for the whole leaf.** The proof rings put the grey on 11 and the wood on 13, beside us. Here the wood is on 10, so the grey's file stands **between** the wood's and ours: "between us was the breath" (r1), "the wood needed the breath, the breath needed the wood" (r6) and "the breath held up between them" (r14) are all read off the same geometry.
3. **The heart stays plain** (§3, A9), with exactly the substitutes A9 lists for II.2: ring 1's braid is two plain runners; ring 2's naming pocket is NAME runners to the named marks. The first bind is in ring 5, the braid and the band-gap in ring 6, the first pocket in ring 9, the blind in ring 12.
4. **The fine devices are the leaf's own sentences**, as §18 and §7 planned them: "one life, in two kinds" a bind (r5); "the wood needed the breath …" the three-strand braid `abcabc` (r6); "without the breath" a gap in the MIST band (r6); "the two are one word" a bind of saying and carving (r11); "the thunder is for the moment, the softness for always" the saying once against a memory ray (r11); "we did not know why" the blind (r12); "we wove the breath thicker" the band widening from one year to the next (r13).
5. **Names are names.** *Naelear* (r1), *Naelsaen* and the shore's *Mystholder* (r9) and *Ael'thar* (r11) are carved as the canon cuts names (the kind-arc in the grey; a heart in the grey; a said-pocket), and the Book's glosses of them ("the mist-hearts", "those of the breath") are Halyna's and Seren's, not cut.
6. **What is not cut**, each a small loss and each flagged in its ring: "thrown down" and "deep" in the fern of r4 (a ligature holds two signs); "a stand of them" in r8 (the choir is the stand); "ever" in r9; "so long" in r13. Halyna's framing ("Here is our fault") is not cut, by §11.2.

**Findings for the spec and the validator** (none blocks anything).

- **A band's year is its knowing's first mark's year** (IV.4's finding) bit again in ring 5, "the grey that was our life": written as one knowing, the MIST band fell in the trees' year and missed our `LIVE`. Fixed by writing the band with its own mark. IV.4's proposal (a note in A8, and a validator warning when a band's own intended mark falls outside its year) stands, with a second instance.
- **Six `@f` terminals had no free cell once packed** (R15): the validator caught every one, and each now runs to its mark (A4). An author writing in knowing form cannot see the packing, so it is worth a line in A8: "write `@f`, run the validator, and move any R15 terminal to the mark".
- **A pocket takes the year its files' cursors stand in**, so its files must hold no mark in that year (K06). P2 first lay over the pillars' file. §8 says "above the highest cursor among them"; the validator reads "the year the cursor is in". **Proposed:** say so in §8, or make the packer move the pocket to the next year.
- **Seven compounds the spec already names are missing from the validator's table**, and two were read wrongly: `DEEP+TREE×3` (the deep forest) as *Yew*, and `BEND+HULL×3` (the twisted boughs) as "bending host". They are proposed in `wf8/additions/W01_grain.md` with six more (§5.2).
- **`!~X` reads "seem to not X"**, where the register used it for "not mis-X" (*must not be mistaken* `!~HOLD`, *no promise misheard* `!~HEAR`). *Settled at the merge* (`wf8/grain_v2_full.md` A17, with W02, W03 and W05's findings): the drawing cannot show the order of `!` and `~`, so `!~X` stays "a seeming not-X", and rings 10 and 11 now cut `!TURN+HEAR` and `!TURN+HOLD`. **`>TRUE`** ("made sure", the register) reads "are true": the causative drops out of a quality's reading.

**Orrowen.**

- **No word was coined.** The frame lands on canon, reserve and existing tier-3 words; the one addition is the set phrase *arra sa re lumm*, "the Kneeling Guest" (§5.1), built on the canon's epithet pattern and IV.4's *et arra*, because the stone text needs the name too (IV.5).
- **Every recurring formula is IV.4's, exactly**: the wood leaves' opening (*Hosen tum Mesk Myst: kethet hy …*), the facing formula (*doss o ul lern …*), "hardest" (*Ew duss sost …*), *hos eth ullen*, the two closing formulas of the headnote, and *Eth re dhess nahos*.
- **"Air" is *hoss*, "breath"**: Orrowen has the sky (*helv*) and the breath, and the wood's air is its breath, the word of the leaf's title.
- **Two forms were tried and dropped**, and are worth a line in the lexicon's findings: *doss o cadhat …* ("it is set …"), because after the pronoun *o* an unmutated word is heard as "its …" (§3.5), which the analyzer enforces for any softenable letter (*doss o rellorat* passes only because *r* does not soften); and ***hossan***, "the breath-folk", because *-an* on a verb stem collides with the first dual (*hossan*, "we two breathe"). The collective *-an* is safe only on stems that are not verbs (*mystan, Orrowan*).

**What I changed for the newcomer (by the critique's numbers).**

2. *Glossing the same things again and again.* The Long Hearth is glossed once, in the note. 3. *The note gave away the climax.* The note now says only that the Captain *raised a banner on the broken wall*. The foreword says he *raised it there on the evening we first came to this Keep*. The cloak and the writing are left for Kael to tell.
4. *The dark-stone paragraph* is put back in order. First the chest. Then the carved stones no one can read, *the mute stones*. 5. *What a bonded pair is.* The note now says: *two people laid in one cradle as babies, who have lived and worked as one ever since*.

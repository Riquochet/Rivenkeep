### 1.15 The Original, translated from v3

The Tongues pass that K2 asked for is made. On 2026-10-03 the whole Book v3.0.0 was set down in its own tongues in nineteen units at once: S01 to S11 for the stone leaves and the Book's furniture (the title page, the Contents, the Books' heads and the Book of Knowings), and W01 to W08 for the eight wood leaves. The units were then merged into one lexicon, one grain and one Book, as the Tier 3 merge did for v1.3 ([the Tier 3 report](Research/Rivenkeep_Tongues_Tier3_Report.md)). The figures are the merge report's (`wf12/merge_report.md`). The *Original* tab is not yet on the story page. It is built from the merged units next, and until then §5.8 holds.

**Back-translation.** Every unit's native lines and rounds were read back blind into English and scored on the pilots' scale: *exact*; *close*, where a nuance shifts; *drift*, where a proposition is lost or changed; and *wrong*. The units mended what drifted and read it again.

Before the mending 1,315 of 1,430 read exact or close (92%); after it, 1,428 (99.9%), and none wrong. The two drifts left are not the merge's: one is a reader's slip in IV.5 where the text stands, and the other is the Guest's sentence in IV.4, whose round is a canon sample and is not re-cut (A34, below). The *after* scores are the fixers' own, by the blind reader's method, and not a second blind pass. The lines the merge itself changed have not been read blind.

**Still open, for Jack.** Each is applied as the merge recommends, and each can be undone.
1. **Three names, romanised by the tongue's rules.** Jory, Wick and Merrick are *Yory*, *Wik* and *Merrik* in the Orrowen, for [Orrowen](Research/Rivenkeep_Tongues_Orrowen_Spec_v2.md) has no *j* and writes a closing *-ck* as *-k*. The English keeps the Book's spellings.
2. **The runners' oath closes with *Ston.*** in I.3 and IV.3, as Kael's oaths close. The English has no word for it, and both can go without moving anything else.
3. **Three plays given up for one rendering:** the hood put back, the axemen home on a spar, and the strip bound on the pike now say what the other leaves say. Any play can come back, in every place at once.
4. **The ring cap (A26).** IV.2, IV.4 and V.6 have 25 story paragraphs in v3, and the units kept to 24 rings. Raise the cap, or keep it.
5. **The Guest's sentence (A34),** the one drift left in the wood: read the canon's first samples by their own band rule, or count their file lines.
6. **The native captions.** Twenty lines of the Book's native furniture stand in English, and nine are given in Orrowen. One rule for the Original either way.
7. **Smaller:** the homophone *miskhovv* (boot) and *biskhovv* (hood); the grain rules marked *proposed*, and fifteen findings for the validator, which wait on a spec pass; and the debts the Tier 3 report left open.

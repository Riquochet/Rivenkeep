### 3 · What I declined, and why

- **Jack's-eye 5.2, *In one ink.* for the Epilogue Argument.** It is a lovely turn of the epithet. I used fidelity's *What the grey sent back.* If the orchestrator prefers the turn, it could go in the Epilogue's body instead. It is already there, in g11's *I write this alone, in one ink*.
- **Jack's-eye Problem 3, *Forgetting chose our fathers.*** I bettered it rather than taking it. This Book trains the reader in object-first order (*Twelve were we*, *The folk behind the grey we call the Mystaeri*, and the very next sentence, *Remembering we choose*). In that order the line parses as *our fathers chose forgetting*, which is the opposite of memory theme 1. *Forgetting came on our fathers, and none chose it.* keeps the mirror and cannot be misread.

  - the vow sits next to the drawing that shows it;
  - the pairs paragraph ends near {{Idrenna}}'s nod *when a tale is laid true*, which hands straight on to the *laid* pun.
  
  Every other part of the re-ordering is applied.
- **Jack's-eye Problem 2, *Never yet were {{Orvenna}} … heard to raise their voices*.** I declined this for budget. The trait keeps its fixed form (voice §4), and the opening carries the extra old grammar instead.
- **Jack's-eye item 11, a budget of 765–808.** I landed at 839, inside fidelity's 830–840. The leaf carries more elements than v2.0's foreword, as voice §7 requires of a leaf near its ceiling. Every further cut on the list would cost a beat:

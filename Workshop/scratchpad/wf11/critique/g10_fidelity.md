# g10 · VI.3 The Knowing of the Green Wood · fidelity critique

*Checked against R6b §VI.3 (a)–(h) and §0.1, outline §7.12, §7.13 and §8 VI.3, the reckoning §2.9 and its datelines table, v2.0 ll. 2521–2697, voice_v3, craft, and the neighbouring drafts as they stand now: I.2 `g02`, II.1 and II.3 `g04`, IV.3 `g06`, IV.5 and V.1 `g07`, V.2 `_g08/V2_book.md`, IV.2 and IV.6 `_w01/`, V.4, V.6 and VI.2 `w02`, the foreword `g01`, I.5 `_g03/I5.md`, and the Epilogue `g11`. Line numbers below are g10.md's.*

**Verdict.** The custom mend holds, and the leaf is clean on every lock. Twelve text fixes follow. Two of them matter most. The first is the seed passage (fix 1): craft forbids the quotation set beside *So it was seed*, and the word *sky*, which the sending is about, never appears in the leaf. The second is Tarnard's line (fix 2), whose recast breaks its echo of IV.6. Three fixes put back elements that the outline or cast planted and the Epilogue relies on (fixes 3, 5 and 6).

## Fixes, most important first

1. **L54–56, the seed passage.**
   - *What:* replace L54 with: **Since the iron came, the pillars had put up no growth. They had waited for the sky to come again.** Leave L56 *So it was seed.* as it is.
   - *Why:*
     - craft §4.2 is binding: *So it was seed*, "with no quotations around it". The draft keeps one quotation (*The iron will tire, the wood said to us once…*) directly before it, and *the wood said to us once* is the cross-reference craft §2.3 and §6.3 cut.
     - The eucatastrophe (outline §7.13: the sending "carries the seed of their sky home to be planted") needs the sky. *Sky* is now absent from the prose, and *to be planted* is cut.
     - The recast is VI.2's own line (w02 l.237) moved into Seren's third person, so it pays VI.2 whole. *Come again* pays IV.2 (`_w01/IV2.md` l.64, *the wood will come again*), and *sky* pays V.4's *grow the sky back*.
     - Its antecedent is the plural *the pillars*. The draft's *any pillar … They* would read as a singular *they*.
     - It saves 9 words and removes the *-eth* *stoppeth*.
   - *If the orchestrator keeps the IV.2 quotation instead,* put `<!-- W: the italic line only -->` before L54. The builder colours the wood's words only by marker, and v2.0's three lines had one. The IV.2 form already matches `_w01/IV2.md`.

2. **L74, Tarnard's line.**
   - *What:* change it to **"Wood that knew the sea knoweth the way home."**
   - *Why:*
     - The draft's *knoweth … will know* turns a known thing into a promise. It also breaks the echo of IV.6, *Even charred, even dying, the wood knoweth the way home.* (`_w01/IV6.md` l.40). R6b's IV.6 seed runs "the wood knows the way home → VI.3 (Tarnard's line) → the Epilogue".
     - R6b (d) lists Tarnard's line among the fixed lines, so keep as much of it as HEAVY allows. This form ends on IV.6's exact four words, keeps one *-eth* and keeps the hinge plain.
     - Change the ask in writer's note §4 to the Epilogue to match. `g11` does not quote the line now.

3. **L84, Seren's lamp at the cutting.**
   - *What:* change *The lamp burned down a finger's width.* to **I held the lamp, and it burned down a finger's width.**
   - *Why:*
     - The Epilogue's halt hangs on this. `g11` has *Twice had I held the lamp to it: … and when it cut the same mark across this stem*, and then *I knew the bite.*
     - v2.0 had *and I held the lamp*, and voice §4 makes holding the lamp Seren's habit.
     - The sentence also becomes a whole echo of V.1, where Seren holds the lamp on the shingle and it burns down a finger's width (`g07`).
     - It adds 4 words.

4. **L78, the silent plank.**
   - *What:* change *every one had spoken to us once* to **all but one had spoken to us once.**
   - *Why:*
     - The draft contradicts itself two lines later (*and the silent one*), and it contradicts V.2 (`_g08/V2_book.md` l.13, *The silent one*). Outline §7.12 says half a heart holds half a knowing.
     - v2.0 had the same slip.
     - The fix becomes Seren's turn: the one plank that never spoke is half of the stem.

5. **After L82, the hull keyed like a wall.** *(This was lost.)*
   - *What:* add one sentence as its own paragraph after *The broken grain met, and held.*: **Every joint after it {{Orvenna}} keyed the same, that no sea should work it loose.**
   - *Why:*
     - The element is in bold in outline §8 VI.3 scene 12, it is R6b beat 17, and v2.0 has *so that no sea could work it loose*.
     - It turns *there is no Stonewright's boat* into one, and it earns *It was small, and it was sound.*
     - The *that … should* clause is the IV.4 construction.
     - Keep it to this one sentence, because R6b (f) warns against a craft log. It may go just before L106 instead, as the cause of *took no water*.

6. **L66, Tarnard's keel.** *(This was lost.)*
   - *What:* add **The keel he laid himself.** after *Tarnel's father.*
   - *Why:*
     - `cast.md` plants it three times (ll.353, 867, 964: Tarnel's knot "ties the bow of the sending, whose keel his father lays"), and outline §8 scene 12 has *Tarnard … laid the keel*.
     - The father's keel and the son's knot at the bow are a pair. Without the keel, Tarnard only takes planks and leaves a lashing.
     - It adds 5 words.

7. **L35, the groves, and the V.1 echo.**
   - *What:* change *the groves sing* to **as the groves sang it**, and *as though one had put it* to **as though someone had put it**.
   - *Why:*
     - V.6 has *Over us the groves sang this as we grew* (w02 l.198), and by VI.2 the groves are the last few, tended by scattered souls. The present *sing* asserts a living Naelear practice that Seren cannot know, which is a grain of invented lore. v2.0 has *sang*.
     - V.1 reads *as though someone had put the word into his mouth* (`g07`), and the writer's own §4 asks V.1 to keep *someone*. Mirror it exactly.

8. **L50, Seren's three sayings.**
   - *What:* change *Thrice I said it ere I wrote it, and it held.* to **Three times I said it over ere I wrote it, and it held.** Better still, set it before the *Mystaeri* sentence, so that *it* is plainly *Naelear*.
   - *Why:*
     - Craft §3.6 makes this the echo of V.1's whole saying (*I said it over three times … It held.*), so it should use V.1's words.
     - *Thrice* is not in the §2.1 stock and appears nowhere else in the Book, which makes it a one-off (§2.4).
     - Placed where it is, *it* can be read as *Mystaeri*.

9. **L160, the song's frame line.**
   - *What:* change *They call it* The Two Homes to **We call it** The Two Homes.
   - *Why:* with *as the Rivenmen sing it still* cut, *They* has nothing to refer to, because *the hearth sang* is singular. Seren is of the hearth.

10. **L58, the fisher-girl.**
    - *What:* restore **a fisher-girl of the east keep**, which adds 4 words. Or tell the Epilogue writer to drop the phrase.
    - *Why:*
      - `g11` l.68 brings her in as *a fisher-girl of the east keep, the great-granddaughter of old Ebba* and then says *It was the same child.*, so the phrase reads as a callback to this leaf.
      - Outline §8 scene 18 has *from the east keep*.

11. **L29, one hand.**
    - *What:* change it to **One hand only had {{Halyna}} needed on any wood since the first winter.**
    - *Why:*
      - With the fronted *Since …*, the inverted *had {{Halyna}} needed* reads first as a conditional (*had they needed…*).
      - This sentence carries R6b's must-survive "one hand needed, two put out". The fix uses the object-first order of voice §1.4.

12. **L142, the ship-masters.**
    - *What:* change *there is naught on the water they fear more* to **…the ship-masters fear more.**
    - *Why:* *they* comes two sentences after *the old ship-masters*, with Merrick (*he*) between. Naming them keeps L144's *They did not look away.* clean.

13. **Cross-tale: the coin's fixed phrase.**
    - *What:* II.1 (`g04` l.25) reads *trust, sealed in stone*, and VI.3 (L108) reads *trust sealed in stone*.
    - *Why:* R6b (d) lists the phrase as fixed, so it needs one form Book-wide. The orchestrator picks the form, and the other leaf matches it.

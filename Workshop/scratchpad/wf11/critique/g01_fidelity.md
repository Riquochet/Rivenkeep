## Fixes, most serious first

**1. Of This Book, line 124: restore "every scribe before me was of a pair". It has fallen between two drafts.**
- *What:* the draft folds the outline's "keep" line, *Every scribe of the guild before me was one of a bonded pair*, into the bare question *Why the Book is in such a hand…*. Its notes (line 347, and §4 line 392) say II.3 must carry it.
- *Why it must be fixed:* g04's II.3 has cut the same line, and its notes (g04 line 224) say *"every scribe before her was one of a pair. The foreword says both."* So **neither leaf says it.** The foreword's question then has nothing under it, because the reader cannot tell why an unbonded hand is strange. The line also grounds the Epilogue's *I am the first scribe of the guild to outlive the tellers* (g11 line 46). Outline §8, foreword beat 7, marks it "keep".
- *Mend (about +10 words):* *Every scribe of the guild before me was one of a pair. Why this Book is in mine, {{Halyna}} tell the children in its place.* Pay for it as in fix 7.

**2. Of This Book, line 128: the names rule is too broad, and later leaves break it.**
- *What:* *Where the wood gave a name of its own, I write that, and not ours.*
- *Why:* as written, it binds every leaf in Seren's hand, and the Book then breaks it more than once:
  - g10's VI.3 (line 50) gives the people's own name, *Naelear*, and says *Mystaeri is but the word of the shore*. Yet g11 still writes *the council-hall of the Mystaeri* in the Epilogue (line 60) and *the Mystaeri cut our words* in the Knowings (line 201).
  - The wood gives the Guest his own name, *Aelrhen* (w02 uses it), but Seren goes on calling him *the Guest*: g10's VI.3 (line 128, *the first step of the Guest's rite*), g11's Epilogue (lines 92 and 124), and w01's II.2 headnote (*the Kneeling Guest's gift*), which comes in the fifth winter.
  - Seren's own headnotes on wood leaves use the wall's names: *this Blackthorn heart* (w01, IV.2).
  
  A rule the Book announces and then breaks is the kind of muddle Jack named. Craft §3.5 asks only for the Thrones by their own names, and VI.2, where they are named, is a wood leaf. w02 (its note 2) relies on the foreword to carry that rule.
- *Mend (−1 word):* *Where the wood speaketh, I write its own names for things, and not ours.* This is true in the third summer and covers VI.2's Thrones. It matches the standing rule that the wood holds only what the wood could know, and leaves Seren's headnotes, the annals, the Epilogue and the Knowings free to keep *Mystaeri*, *the Kneeling Guest* and *Blackthorn*.
- *If the orchestrator would rather keep the broad rule:* g11 must write *Naelear* for *Mystaeri* in the Epilogue and in the Knowings' closing note, and Seren's leaves after the wood names him (VI.3, the Epilogue, the late headnotes) must write *Aelrhen*. That is a larger change across several drafts.

- Books Two and Four, the Epilogue and the Appendix match.
- *Why:* the Contents copies the headings, so the built Book would show two different Arguments for the same Book.
- *Mend:* the orchestrator picks one text for each of these Books, and both files change to match.
  - All six candidates are one line and spoil nothing.

**4. Contents, line 73, and the g11 Epilogue heading: the Epilogue's Argument spoils the ending.**
- *What:* *The tellers dead, one scribe left, and a grey boat come back.* The draft writer kept it and flagged it (line 404).
- *Why:* the reader meets the Contents before the first tale, and this line gives away the Book's last death (D12, {{Halyna}}; the Epilogue's *{{Halyna}} are gone.*) and the deaths of the other tellers. Craft §3.5: the Arguments "are one line each and spoil nothing."
- *Mend:* change g01 line 73 and g11 line 3 together. Two options:
  - *What the grey sent back.* This answers Book Six's *what was sent*.
  - *A grey boat come back, and what it bare.* This uses *bare*, which is in the stock.
  
  Do not use the draft's own fallback, *One scribe left…*: it still tells the reader the others are gone.

**5. Of This Book, line 108: match Ebba's habit to the words the later leaves pay.**

- *Why:* g03's I.5 (*most nights she began it, a breath before the rest*) and g10's VI.3 (*began the answer a breath before the rest*) both pay the plant with *a breath before the rest*. g03's notes (line 336) say outright that the foreword must plant *a breath before the rest*. The foreword is the plant, so the words should ring true when they come back.

**6. Of This Book, line 132: an element of v2.0 is lost without being logged: *the last word is still in my mouth*.**

- *Mend (+2 words):* *Most nights the coals are ash, and the last word yet in my mouth. So it is tonight.*

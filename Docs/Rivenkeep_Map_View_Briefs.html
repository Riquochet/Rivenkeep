Rivenkeep — Map View Art Direction Briefs

GDD v5.1.5 companion file. These are mood boards for the flat, color-coded "Map View" used in Build & Deploy phases — the WWII command table aesthetic. Not final designs. Reference only.

The Three Layers

Every battlefield is assembled from three design layers collapsed into one compressed file.

Layer 1 — Base Map

Permanent geography. One per theater. 10 total. Castle positions, zone boundaries, theater identity.

Layer 2 — Environmental Overlay

Terrain puzzle. One per battle. 190 total. Rocks, trees, cliffs, fragile grids, DMZ position.

Layer 3 — Enemy Overlay

Combat puzzle. One per battle. 190 total. Spawn points, ship assignments per sortie, approach vectors. Deterministic AI.

Theater Mood Boards — Map View (Build/Deploy)

All 10 theaters at full detail. Each shows: open space (3 color variants), trees, rocks, cliffs, 5 castles with wall enclosures and cannons, DMZ line, spawn points, and ships. These are the flat, color-coded Map View — not the rich 2.5D World View used in Fight phase.

1. Coastal Theater

Harbour walls, open water, beach landings — the first theater the player encounters.

2. River Theater

Winding waterways split the land — island strongholds, fragmented build space, bridge crossings.

3. Swamp Theater

Scattered solid ground amid murky bog — tight build space, dense vegetation, isolated castles.

4. Forest Theater

Dense canopy, narrow clearings — castles hide in glades between ancient trees.

5. Mountain Theater

High ridges, narrow passes, heavy cliffs — chokepoints define every battle.

6. Volcanic Theater

Lava rivers split the map — dark rock terrain, fragile ground, isolated build islands.

7. Desert Theater

Wide open sand, sparse oases — few natural defenses, wind drift dominant.

8. Frozen Theater

Ice shelves, frozen channels, snow ground — terrain splits and re-freezes.

9. Sky Theater

Floating platforms over void — narrow bridges are the only connections.

10. Crystal Theater

Crystal spires reflect shots — Mirror Cannon's home theater, geometry is everything.
